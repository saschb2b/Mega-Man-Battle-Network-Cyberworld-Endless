# Other games' ROMs

* [Other games' ROMs](overview.md) - Optional ROMs of other Battle Network games, BN5 first: what their data lends a run (net areas, music, virus families, Navis, chips, Souls), how it moves into BN6's engine, and where it stands.
* [The chips' code](chips-code.md) - Issue #82's C2 and C3: BN5's chips proved against BN5's own battle frame by frame, and BN5's code for those BN6 lacks moved in as units (`tools/xchiprecipe.py`, `src/emu/xchips_code.c`), DarkInvs and Program Advances among them.
* [Data, not code](data-not-code.md) - BN4, BN5 and BN6 share formats, so another game's maps, songs, sprites and chip tables play in BN6 as data, its code only once moved; each pool a second ROM fills and its cost.
* [How it is built](how-it-is-built.md) - Extra ROMs found by game code and SHA-1 (`src/core/rom.c`), their maps learned and tile sets copied into BN6's free space, areas dressed by `run_dress`, songs copied by `xsong.c`, and the extra ROMs offered on each platform (BN4's held behind its research switch).
* [Guest battles](guest-battles.md) - History of BN5's battles fought on a second mGBA core (`src/emu/guest.c`, epic #57) before issue #82 moved every battle into BN6's engine; the guest core now serves only as the differential's reference, BN5's battles and BN4 Red Sun's.
* [One net: the design](one-net.md) - Issue #82's design for one Battle Network: every battle in BN6's engine, BN5's territories keeping their identity with visitors at the seams, Souls in every battle, DarkChips at one price, and its rounds as built.
* [BN5's music in a run](music.md) - Where a run plays BN5's songs through BN6's music player (`src/audio/xsong.c`): Before the Boss 0x1C at a guardian's prelude on BN5's areas, Area Liberated 0x18 and One-Turn Win 0x24 on dark layers.
* [The chip registry](chip-registry.md) - BN5's 257 chips in BN6's chip tables grown to 512 (`src/core/xchips_registry.c`, `chips.c`): a chip both games name stays BN6's, the rest take free ids, with placeholders without BN5's ROM and the pack extended in EWRAM.
* [Battle Network 4 in a run](bn4.md) - What BN4 Red Sun's ROM could lend a run (measured), what players said of BN4 and what follows from it, the design checked against the core loop (BN4's Navis and viruses first, its tournament as an act's climax), and a phased plan with the owner's open calls.
* [BN4's Navis in BN6's engine](bn4-navis.md) - What moving BN4 Red Sun's eighteen new Navis into BN6's battle engine takes, Navi by Navi: how each runs (two builds, the Soul Navis on MegaMan's own states), what it calls and spawns against BN6's twins, the order by cost, the engine routines to port once and what the Navi recipe generator needs for BN4.
* [BN4's Souls on BN6's proxy form](bn4-souls.md) - What moving BN4 Red Sun's twelve Souls (Red Sun's six and Blue Moon's six, which run in Red Sun's ROM) onto BN6's proxy form takes: how BN4 takes a form, each Soul's abilities and the code it brings against BN6's twins, what BN5's path supplies and what BN4 must, a cost ranking and which Souls first.
* [Status](status.md) - Checklist of what BN5's ROM brings a run: done, its areas dressing BN6's, music, bystanders, battles, chips, Navis and Souls in BN6's engine, the 3DS; open, its towns, Team ProtoMan, BN4 (researched) and Falzar.
* [Transplanted Navis](navis.md) - Issue #82's Navi track: BN5's Team Colonel Navis, the Dark Soul and two Darkloids moved into BN6's battle engine (`src/emu/xnavi_install.c`, `tools/xnavirecipe.py`), BN6's Navi tables grown, each proved frame by frame by `build.py xdiff`.
* [Transplanted Souls](souls.md) - Issue #82's Souls track: BN5's six Team Colonel Souls in BN6's engine on the proxy form 8, milestones S0-S7 from UNITE on BN6's Custom screen to Chaos Unison and BN5's mood (`src/emu/soul*.c`, `tools/xsoul.py`).
* [Transplanted families](families.md) - Issue #82's virus families moved from BN5 into BN6's battle engine (`src/emu/xvirus.c`): generated recipes (`tools/xrecipe.py`), the all-or-nothing install, the engine's grown tables and second-level dispatch, and all 25 families proved by `build.py xdiff`.
