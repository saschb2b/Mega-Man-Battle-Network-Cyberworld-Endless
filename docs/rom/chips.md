| Chip records (0x2C bytes) | `0x021DA8` | Same address as Falzar's `ChipDataArr`. Cannon = 40 power, codes A B C *. Codes at 0, rarity 0-4 at 5, library type at 7 (0 standard, 1 Mega, 2 Giga, 3 secret, 4 Program Advance), attack family at 0xB (27 the Navi chips, 81 the Dragons, 73 MchnSwrd's line, 21 placed objects and traps, 36 TankCan1-3: chips 12-14, power 120, 160 and 200, which fire after a wind-up), lock-on setting at 0xF (1 for chips that strike an enemy outright, Cannon, the swords, bombs; 0 for traps, counters, MchnSwrd's paralysed-only cut, VarSwrd's key inputs, GolmHit's and TimeBom's delays, and the Navi chips and Dragons; checked against those chips), library number at 0x15, library flags at 0x16 (bit 0: the other version's Navi chips and Otenko; 0x30: the arm chips and the five DarkChips the US version cut, whose 0x20 keeps a chip out of the pack's list ([BN6's own DarkChips](chips-code.md)); 0x10 alone: the Beast chips), and two picture pointers at 0x24 and 0x28, which the chips cut from the US version share as one blank (`0x086F3AF0`, the same word in both). A run can find or buy 249 of them (`src/core/chip_pool.c`): standard 1-202, the Megas 221-274 but Falzar's Navis, the ten Gigas 301-310. HackJack, HackJck EX and HackJck SP (275-277, Megas of a Navi the Japanese version has) pass every rule but the blank picture: taken from an official vault, one used in battle made MegaMan vanish (his Navi's sprite is a white dot in the US version, sprite category 0x3F) until he was deleted (issue #15). Verify by listing the records whose words at 0x24 and 0x28 are equal: 34, all left out by the other rules but 275-277. Records 203-220 are blank, 315 on pseudo-chips. |

| Chip Traders (`src/layer/trader.c`) | `0x086C3758` TextScriptChipTrader, `0x0804CDE0` prize pools, `0x0809B7E4` trader kinds | The trader archive is stored uncompressed; script 0 is the Chip Trader (3 chips), 6 the Chip Trader Special (10), 12 the BugFrag Trader, each with the game's Yes/No, not-enough lines and "Try again?". The trade screen (`selectChipTraderRewardList`, Gregar `0x0804CDB4`) looks the current map (group << 8 \| number) up in five 12-byte entries (map key, chip list of 6-byte records ending with 0, weights per rarity): 0x104 70 chips of rarity 1-3, 0x501 130 of 1-4, 0x000 108 of 1-3, 0x400 163 mostly 3-4, 0x9301 60 of 3-5 for BugFrags. With no match it returns no list and draws garbage prizes. `0x0809B7BC` looks the map up in six 8-byte entries (map key, byte) for the trader's script, which picks the lines the screen shows; with no match it takes the map number. Each layer rewrites the second entry of both tables (Sky Town's `0x501`, a map no run visits) with its own map; the first stays AsterLand's `0x104`, whose own Chip Trader stands at home (issue #96), its list's records BN6's own. Falzar's tables are at `0x0804C01C` and in `asm03_2.s`. The prize itself (bn6f `sub_804BD00`): two weights at `0x0804BDCC` (`C0 40`, Falzar's `dword_804B008` 0xDC4 later, as the reward list routine is; the pool weights `70 60 18 10 08` follow) choose between the pool's chips whose Library flag (`0x1E20` + chip id) is set and those whose is not, falling back to the other when one is empty; then a rarity by the pool's weights (fallback orders at `0x0804BDF0`) and a chip of it by `GetRNG`. Layers write `00 40`: new chips first. A list record is 6 bytes: the chip id (u16), then up to four codes as the chip data has them (0-25 A-Z, 26 *, `0xFF` ends); the prize's code (bn6f `sub_804BF18`, Gregar `0x0804CCDC` by `tools/bn6f_match.py`'s map) is one of its record's codes, one the pack holds none of three times in four: two weights at `0x0804BE2C` (`C0 40`, read by the literal at `0x0804CD50`; Falzar's `byte_804B068`), the first the codes the pack lacks. Since issue #154 the layers' and home's traders keep their pools' records as BN6 has them, so that pick spreads a run's codes as BN6 spreads them ([Economy](../game/economy/overview.md)); before, they rewrote the records in the core's copy to the folder's code and kept only those, which gave every run's trades its folder's letters. Verify by reading the two bytes at `0x0804BE2C`, by dumping a pool's first records beside the chips' own codes at `chip_data` (the same bytes, the core's copy as the file's), and by trading at a layer's trader three times with a pack of one code: most prizes come in another. |
