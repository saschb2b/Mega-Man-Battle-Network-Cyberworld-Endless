| Shops (`src/layer/shop.c`) | `0x046B68` shop descriptors, `0x047D70` initial shop data, eToolkit+0x54 ShopDataPtr | A descriptor is 16 bytes: currency (0 zenny, 1 BugFrags, 2 Chip Order), keeper text, offset into the shop data, entries. Entries are 8 bytes: kind (1 item: 0x70 HPMemory, 0x80+ SubChips; 2 chip; 3 NaviCust program), stock (0xFF unlimited), u16 id, code (program color), 0, u16 price in hundreds. The shop screen lists the entries with stock left (a last one bought, the keeper says "We're all sold out!" over an empty list), and empties the list unless each entry's id and code appear among that shop's entries in the ROM's initial data (bn6f `sub_80473E6`), so layers write their stock to both; the text command `EF 22 shop stocked soldout` (ts_check_shop_stock) jumps by whether any entry has stock left; shops 0 (the Net Dealer, keeper face 0x3E) and 3 (the NaviCust vendor, keeper face 0x42, zenny, 8 entries: descriptor `0x046B98`) are taken over. Shops 6 and 7 (Undernet 2's, BN6's BugFrag shops: chips, and programs at 20 to 100) have currency 1, their prices a count of BugFrags where currency 0's are hundreds of zenny; the NaviCust vendor's shop 3 is given currency 1 in the core's copy (`shop.c` vendor_bugfrags, the descriptor's first byte at `0x046B98`, where the player's ROM holds 0), and BN6's screen then shows his prices and the BugFrags held and takes BugFrags (`SHOP_BUGFRAGS` in `src/layer/shop.h`; verify: `python3 tools/play.py start v --fresh --seed 2640393 -- --scene emu --run-depth 2 --dev quiet --talk bugfrags:1900,programs:2500`, then `do v "mash A 1200; wait 1150; press A; wait 40; press A; wait 40; press A; wait 40; press A; wait 60"`: his list priced in BugFrags beside the 50 held (SuprArmr 9, MegFldr1 8, Custom1 11, Attack+1 6, BlzrdBal H 15, checked 26 October 2026), SuprArmr bought leaving 41, the chip 26). Shop 3's keeper text (`0x7F0420`, its descriptor's text pointer) is LZ77; its decompressed text says `F5 00 42` (the orange technician navi's face) seven times, the first from a literal at `0x7F046A` (`BN6_VENDOR_FACE`), the other six back-references that copy it, so that one byte, 0x45 in the core's copy, gives the window the vendor's own GirlNavi face (`vendor_face`). Verify: decompress the text with and without the byte changed: exactly seven bytes differ, each a face; a program shop's window then shows the face its chat does. The layers' picks come from the initial data as the player's ROM holds it, never from the core's rewritten copy, so a layer's stock does not depend on the layers built before it. Traced in `tools/romlab`. |
