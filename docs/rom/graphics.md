| Floor sprites (`src/layer/npc.c`) | NPC commands `0x1B`, `0x0C` | Overworld sprites share MegaMan's layer priority, which coordinate data section 2 sets per position (`checkLayerPriority_80318b0`), and the game orders them in OAM front to back by depth. `ns_set_layer_priority_override_to_3` puts a pad at priority 3, under MegaMan and over the floor layers. That alone fails from behind: between sprites the GBA draws the earlier OAM entry whatever their priority bits, so a pad sorted in front of MegaMan took the pixels over his feet and then lost them to the floor layer. Pads therefore stand 64 units further back in depth (+X, -Y) and 64 lower, which draws them in the same place but sorts them after MegaMan, and `ns_shift_center` (x, y, z signed bytes, stored in the object's CenterOffsetX/Y/Z) moves their collision and talk centre back to the pad. |

| Battle backgrounds (`src/gfx/backdrop.c`, `xbackdrop.c`; RomLayout `battle_bgs`, `battle_bg_anims`, `battle_bg_scroll`, `battle_bg_refs`) | `0x082058` BGAnimData per background, `0x0822E0` animation lists, `0x081EF4` scroll callbacks | bn6f `sub_8080DA0` loads the battle background by id through three tables (Falzar `0x08080F98`, `0x08081220`, `0x08080E34`); in Gregar each sits 0x10C0 bytes later, found by the literal `0x08082058` at `0x082054` and by searching for 7-word BGAnimData records (tiles, VRAM `0x06000020`, map, `0x800`, palette, `0x03001960`, `0x20`). A record's tiles are a word count and the offset of their LZ77 data; the map is width and height bytes (32, 32) with LZ77 at +12; the palette a size word then 16 colours. Ids 0x03, 0x10, 0x14 and 0x15 have no palette and take it from their animation. The animation lists (ending `0xFFFFFFFF`) hold GFXAnim scripts: +0 and +4 parameters, +8 command (0 palette copy: destination, size; 4 tile copy: ROM tiles, VRAM destination, +10 tile count), then (parameter, delay) pairs ending 0 or looping at 1; a tile copy's parameter is a list of u16 tile numbers with flips in bits 10-11, which is how the digit blocks and the Undernet's static move. Each id's scroll is the second word of its 16-byte callback entry: `0x080019B5` diagonal (BG offsets -8/16 and -4/16 a frame), `0x080019ED` up, `0x08001A11` down, `0x08001A35` slow right, `0x08001A59` fast left, `0x080019B1` none. Every id decoded with its animations shows its area's battle background (Central's rings of digits, the Undernet's static, the Aquarium's fish). The loader reads the three tables through literal words, the same three in both its routines (Gregar `0x081E60` and `0x081EAA`) and nowhere else: `0x082054` the BGAnimData table, `0x081EEC` the animation lists, `0x081EF0` the scroll entries; another game's backgrounds go after BN6's 22 in copies of the tables, the words pointed at them (`src/gfx/xbackdrop.c`). A tile animation stages its tiles in the EWRAM buffer its fourth byte names, through the table at `0x08001AB8` (the same in Gregar and Falzar): 0 and most others `0x0200DF40` (1280 bytes before `0x0200E440`, and BN6's own largest animation, 50 tiles, runs on into it), 2 `0x0200E580` (192), 3 `0x0200E640` (2304, 72 tiles), 11 `0x0200E440` (bn6f `ewram.s`).
