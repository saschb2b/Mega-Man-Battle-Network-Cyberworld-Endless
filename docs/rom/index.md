# The ROM map

* [ROM data](overview.md) - Where BN6 Gregar's ROM data lives: every offset is for the USA ROM by its SHA-1 and lives in `RomLayout` (`src/core/rom.c`), found through bn6f and checked in `tools/romlab`; the tables by subsystem.
* [Symbols](symbols.md) - The symbol files written from the project's map of BN6 Gregar and BN5 Colonel (`docs/symbols/`, `tools/symbols.py`): what they hold, how to load them in mGBA or no$gba, how each symbol is verified and what may be reused.
* [Sprites, fonts and pictures](graphics.md) - ROM addresses of BN6's pictures: the sprite pointer lists, fonts, title screen and marks, map and Navi sprites, net and battle backdrops, mugshots, the map-name label and the ROMs screen's faces, each with how it was verified.
* [The game's structures, boot and events](game-state.md) - ROM and RAM addresses the engine drives BN6 through: boot and warps, MegaMan held on the map, the structures read field by field (`src/emu/bn6.h`), map, flag and key item hooks, run locks, gifts and starting state.
* [BN6's battles](battle.md) - Addresses of BN6's battles: enemy ids, stats and traits, the encounter roll and battle hooks, battlefields and panel types, rewards, DeleteTime, the battle loop and banners, Custom gauge, guardians and super bosses.
* [Chips, folders and the Library](chips.md) - Addresses of BN6's chips: the chip records, names and descriptions, Navi chips, folders and chips owned, the Library, Program Advances, the All * helper, Chip Traders and the BugFrag Trader's trade.
* [BN5's Liberation Missions](liberation.md) - Addresses for BN5's Liberation Missions and their board drawn in BN6: the missions and rewards, each mission's look, banners and L view, the Darkloids' phase, BN5's Darkloids, teammates in another Navi's body and the battle's field.
* [The net's maps and their pieces](net-maps.md) - Addresses behind the generated net layers: map takeover, net maps and buffers, warp pads, arrow panels, Rush's gaps, teleports, Link Navi obstacles, purple Mystery Data, height, counters, framed pads, floor shades and emblems.
* [The real world and home](world.md) - Addresses of the real world and home: Lan's HP, Lan's house, AsterLand and the Cyber Academy, the requests' scripts, home's shops and the Order Service's sort, real-world maps, objects, warps and music.
* [Scripts, text and shops](scripts-and-shops.md) - Addresses of BN6's NPC and text scripts, its shops and the talking reach that the engine's layer objects use (`src/layer/npc.c`, `text.c`, `shop.c`).
* [Sound and music](sound.md) - Addresses of BN6's sound: its MP2K engine that the engine's own player follows, the song and player tables, the music and effects silenced, and settings.ini's sound mix.
* [The NaviCust](navicust.md) - Addresses of the NaviCust: its programs, compression and compression codes, the board, Reg memory and RegUps, the TagChip system and the Spins found in the net.
* [The PET and the second screen](pet-and-second-screen.md) - Addresses behind the PET menu, its labels and KeyItem and E-Mail words, the KeyItem screen's cursor for the NetPass, and every panel the second screen reads (chips, shops, NaviCustomizer, battle, Library, E-Mail).
* [The chip registry and BN5's chips' code](chips-code.md) - Addresses for the chip registry (BN6's chip tables grown and moved, BN5's read) and BN5's chips' code in BN6's engine: the routines the chips' differential read, Program Advances, DarkPlus, support chips and BN6's own DarkChips.
* [Online: the Square, the Arena and the link battle](online.md) - Addresses online play rests on: a Net Square trade settled, its music and name tags, the Arena's end of a NetBattle, the stands' report, two MegaMen a side in BN6's link battle, the link packet and BN6's expressions.
