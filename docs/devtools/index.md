# Developer tools

* [Dev tools](overview.md) - Developer tools that check the game without playing it out: layer atlas, tile test and tour, pacing and economy reports, run log, dev menu, battles on demand, online tests, differential, recipe generators, agents' MCP tools.
* [tools/play.py: a game kept for a playtest](play.md) - `tools/play.py`: one game kept per NAME under `.build/play` for a playtest, replayed from its seed and history, a pinned build, and the dev steps (place, flags, stuck, foes, xrecord, status, battle, sig, second, dump).
* [Nav study: the way across BN6's own maps](nav-study.md) - `--atlas nav` walks each area's original BN6 net maps and counts the one-wide walkways crossed between their farthest panels, setting `narrow_cap`, and `--atlas arrivals` lists where BN6's maps place MegaMan as he arrives.
* [A controller in a test](controller.md) - `--pad KIND` (xbox, playstation, ps5, nintendo, generic) attaches an SDL virtual controller to a headless shot or `tools/play.py` game, and `pad.NAME` and `key.NAME` steps press its buttons and keyboard keys as a player's.
* [The controller's feel: what was sent](feel.md) - `--dev feel` prints each beat, rumble and DualSense trigger effect the controller's feel sends, and `--pad ps5` attaches a virtual DualSense that says what reached SDL, since nobody can feel a controller headless.
* [Pacing report: every act's battles and guardians](pacing-report.md) - `python3 build.py pacing` rolls every area's random battles in every act and draws 500 runs' guardians into `.build/pacing.txt`, marking OVER or OUTSIDE past the act's band, BN5's territories and the encounter groups included, then plays seeds 1-12 for the battles per layer and act, NONE marking a layer without one.
* [Run log](run-log.md) - `runlog.txt` in the data folder (`src/director/runlog.c`) records every battle (seed, depth, area, kind, HP before and after, foes, busting level, reward), each layer left with its random battles, and each run's end with its economy's counts, on the handheld too.
* [The Net Square: two games at once](net-square.md) - Testing the Net Square on a local server: `python3 build.py server-run`, a data folder's `network.ini` or `--dev online=URL`, two `tools/play.py` games, dev steps (`emote`, `netpass`, `trade`, `reconnect`) and `tools/link_relay.py` for dropped links.
* [Atlas: every layer drawn](atlas.md) - `python3 build.py atlas [BIOMES] [SEEDS]` draws every area's layers in its own tiles without running the game: sheets, seam and defect pictures and a report in `.build/atlas`, failing where a layer is worse than `tests/atlas_baseline.txt`.
* [The Navis' recipe generator](navi-recipe-generator.md) - `tools/xnavirecipe.py` derives the Navis' recipes as `tools/xrecipe.py` derives a family's, writing the control HeatMan's, the moved objects' and BN5's Navis' into `src/emu/xnavi_bn5.c`, with `--check` and a `--report`.
* [Frame log: pacing on a player's machine](frame-log.md) - `CYBERWORLD_FRAME_LOG=1` (on the 3DS `frame_log = on` in `settings.ini`) prints a line a second of frames shown and played, the gaps between them and each frame's update, drawing and present times, plus the 3DS's heap.
* [Tile test: clean tiles in every area](tile-test.md) - `python3 build.py tiles [SEEDS]` runs the atlas over every area and layout, lists each area's tiles drawn off and seams per 100 panels with reasons, and fails where a layer got worse than the baseline.
* [The bracket's differential](bracket-diff.md) - `tools/xbracket.py` runs BN4 Red Sun's tournament bracket in romlab and its move into BN6 in the host build from the same entrants, and compares their frames pixel for pixel on the screen's own clock, with mutations of the comparator and of the move.
* [The differential: a moved family against its own game](differential.md) - `python3 build.py xdiff` (`tools/xdiff.py`) fights each `tests/xdiff` spec in BN5's engine and BN6's with the moved family, Navi, chip or Soul, comparing traces frame by frame to PASS or FAIL; `--mutations` tests the comparator.
* [The Arena on one machine](arena.md) - `--dev arena` runs BN6's Practice NetBattle between two linked cores on one machine, with switches for delay, lag, rollback, seeds, desyncs, two MegaMen a side (`arena2v2`) and mutations, and `--dev rollbench` timing a rollback's cost.
* [build.py shot: a headless capture's options](headless-shot.md) - `build.py shot`'s options for a headless capture in the build image: the scenes, run depth, area, guardian, seed, setup and marks, `--talk`, scripted `--input` and `--taps`, and the frames saved.
* [Sprite gallery](sprite-gallery.md) - L and SELECT together on the title screen, or `--scene gallery`, opens a viewer of every ROM sprite's animations, browsed by category, sprite, animation and palette.
* [BN5's records in BN6's engine](bn5-records.md) - `python3 build.py xrecords [--all]` (`tools/xrecords.py`) lists every battle record of BN5's areas as BN6's engine fights it or why it cannot, and checks each area's random battles roll the same again within the band.
* [Economy report: what a run hands out](economy-report.md) - `python3 build.py economy [RUNS]` plays 300 runs without running the game and prints per act the chips, Mystery Data, zenny and codes handed out under today's plan and the proposal's, flagging any past its band.
* [Tour: the game shows every room](tour.md) - `python3 build.py tour [BIOMES]` runs the game headless and warps MegaMan through every room of one layer per area, saving `.build/tour/tour_bXX.png` to show objects, NPCs and collision problems the atlas cannot.
* [The recipe generator](recipe-generator.md) - `tools/xrecipe.py` derives the recipes of BN5's 25 virus families BN6 lacks from both ROMs, numbers only, writing `src/emu/xvirus_bn5.c` and `tests/xdiff/families.json`, with `--check` to compare and `--report` for each family.
* [Dev menu: shorter test runs](dev-menu.md) - Select+R opens the dev menu in battle or on the net (can't die, one-hit enemies, speed, next layer), and `--dev` sets those switches and many with no menu entry (`fragile`, `dark`, `souls=MASK`, `folder=ID`, `hp=N`).
* [The 3DS's heap on a computer](3ds-heap.md) - `tools/heap3ds.py` plays long headless runs of the host build with every allocation counted as the New 3DS's 96 MB heap holds it, reporting the peak, refused allocations and run depth, BN6 alone, with BN5, with BN4 Red Sun, or with both.
* [Town and world: the real world](town-and-world.md) - `python3 build.py town [SEEDS]` plans and draws the town each seed's run starts in, unmatched tiles marked red, then tours it; `python3 build.py world` draws and tours the real world's 37 original maps.
* [Statistics: asked, sent and checked](statistics.md) - Only a plain player's start asks about and sends the anonymous statistics; `--dev statistics=ask|on|off` and `statsurl=URL` test the question, Options' line and a local Umami stand-in, and `statscheck` sends one event to Umami itself.
* [The engine's sound against the game's](sound-check.md) - `python3 tools/mp2k_verify.py [SONG...]` lays the engine's MP2K beside BN6's own sound in romlab frame by frame, comparing SoundInfo, channels, registers and the sound for PASS or each first difference, with mute and phase switches; `xN/SONG` lays another game's song, copied into BN6 by xsong.c, beside that game playing it.
* [A battle on demand](battle-on-demand.md) - Dev steps for `--input` and `tools/play.py`: `battle` (the layer's next random battle), `foes` (named enemies at their panels), `lab` (the same foes as the Battle Lab's test battle, inside a snapshot of the game), `swap` (a Navi swapped mid-battle for the next), `xrecord` (a BN5 record), `bracket` (BN4's tournament bracket moved into BN6), `status` (a panel's type too), `chip`, `folder` and `sig`, with `tools/xcontrol.py` and `tools/xfamily.py`'s battle checks.
* [Agents' tools: build, play, capture and compare in a call](agents-tools.md) - The `cyberworld` MCP server (`tools/cw_mcp.py`; `tools/cw.py` from a shell), whose `shot`, `play` and `build` capture a screen by route, keep a playtest, compare commits or build in one call, answering in words and a picture.
* [tools/romlab: the plain ROM for research](romlab.md) - `tools/romlab` runs the plain ROM in libmgba for research (scripted input, memory peeks and pokes, states, recordings, probes on its code and watches on its stores), and `labtrace.py` traces captured tiles, palettes and OBJs back to ROM offsets.
* [The proxy form: where a Soul stands](proxy-form.md) - `--dev soulproxy` makes form 8, Falzar's TenguCross that Gregar never offers, an exact copy of HeatCross for BN5's Souls, and `tools/xproxy.py` proves it by one battle fought four ways, every byte traced.
* [How the switches work](switches.md) - How the dev menu's switches work inside BN6: event flag 0x1700 holding random battles off, HP writes for can't die and one-hit enemies, and extra emulator frames per shown frame for speed.
* [The chip recipe generator](chip-recipe-generator.md) - `tools/xchiprecipe.py` derives the code of BN5's chips from both ROMs and the symbol map, writing `src/emu/xchips_bn5.c`, `xchips_moved.c` and the Program Advances' `xchips_pas.c`, with `--check` and a `--report` of every unit; `tools/xchiprecipe_bn4.py` derives BN4's units into `src/emu/xchips_bn4.c`.
* [The RAM survey: what BN6 leaves alone](ram-survey.md) - `tools/ram_survey.py` runs a battery of headless autopilot games that mark every byte of the GBA's work RAM a frame ended with not zero (`CYBERWORLD_RAM_SURVEY`), prints the stretches that stayed zero, with `--canary` fills a stretch with a pattern and tells every byte a frame changed (`CYBERWORLD_RAM_CANARY`), and with `--watch` tells every read and write the game's code makes of a stretch, the save RAM's too, changing nothing (`CYBERWORLD_RAM_WATCH`): the measurement before the engine claims RAM.
* [Encounter tags and danger](encounter-tags.md) - `tools/encounter_tags.py` tags every virus family in the engine from the ROMs (versions, element, weakness, role, reward row) and counts how each battle template fills per ROM set and act; `tools/encounter_danger.py` fights battles headless for their pressure, tricks and the autopilot's losses.
