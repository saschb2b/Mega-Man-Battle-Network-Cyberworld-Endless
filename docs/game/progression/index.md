# Progression

* [Progression](overview.md) - Progression, how a run's difficulty is decided (`src/core/pacing.c`): depth sets difficulty and the area only flavour, per-act HP budgets and damage caps, guardians by HP band, a fixed heal rhythm, and the research behind it.
* [The structure](structure.md) - The progression structure: depth decides difficulty, the short and endless nets, per-act HP and damage targets, how battles, BN5's records, areas and guardians are chosen, the battle floor, growth, risk the player chooses, the Cybeast, later cycles.
* [What BN6's data says](bn6-data.md) - What BN6's ROM data says about difficulty: virus families' HP and damage by version, each area's battle HP from Central to the Graveyard, guardians' V1, EX and SP HP, and MegaMan's 100 HP start.
* [Measuring it](measuring.md) - How progression is measured: `build.py pacing` rolls every area's battles per act and 500 runs' guardians against their bands and plays seeds 1-12 for the battles per layer and act, `tests/test_core.c` checks the rules, `runlog.txt` records battles, and `build.py economy` measures handouts.
* [What other roguelikes do](other-roguelikes.md) - What other roguelikes do about fair difficulty and what transfers: Into the Breach's easiest first island, One Step From Eden's enemy tiers, Slay the Spire's easy pool and rests, Hades, Balatro, BN4's virus tiers.
* [Where it is decided](where-decided.md) - Where progression is decided in code: acts, bands, versions and heals in `src/core/pacing.c`, area order and guardian pools in `src/core/run.c`, super bosses, formations, BN5's records, visitors, guardian versions, chip rarity and rewards.
* [Status](status.md) - How the built progression differs from the first proposal: SP Navis in Servers from act 4, guardian draws and per-act bands, what is not built, and fixes on the way (Colonel as navi 18).
* [How a run worked before](before.md) - How a run's difficulty worked before the progression research: 19 layers a cycle from 15 areas, virus versions by depth, a random first area, guardian versions, the Recovery Mr. Prog's heal, growth and MegaMan's start.
