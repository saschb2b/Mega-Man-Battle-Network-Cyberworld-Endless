# The Virus Battler's stake (25 October 2026)

BN6's Virus Battler ([The Virus Battler](../../world/level-design/virus-battler.md)) is a wager,
not a find: the owner's decision of 25 October 2026 has a run stake zenny
on a series of BN6's own rounds. It sits outside the acts' sums and bands
as the requests do; `build.py economy` gave 0 of the plan in play outside
its bands with it in (the layers it stands on lay their data as before,
the Battler taking a side path's end after the blue data).

- **Through the game-design skill.** The dialectic is the run's own,
  prepare or press on, restated as push your luck: each choice comes after
  the next pair is shown (a telegraph: input randomness, not output), and
  the loss is the whole pot. It feeds the run's loop (zenny for the act's
  dealer) and the meta loop's variety (the card's viruses, never power
  outside him). One series a visit keeps a strong card from farming him.
- **The stake** is the Battler's own repeat prize in BN6, the unit of his
  series: what a win adds to the pot, so a first win doubles it. The pot
  is the stake and a stake a win: cashing out after w wins pays (w + 1)
  stakes, for w + 1 risked rounds.
- **When to stop.** Fighting on from a pot of w + 1 stakes is worth it
  where the next round is won more often than (w + 1) / (w + 2): two in
  three after the first win, three in four after the second. A card of
  Mettaur and Gunner wins little and should stop early; a grown card plays
  a long series.

The short net's second act has Battler 1, its third Battler 5. About half
the acts hold one (51% of the endless net's, 63% of the short net's, at
least one a run). A run's first series won against a Battler also pays his
BN6 first prize (WindRack \*, HP+500, BugFix \*, FldrPak2, SuprVulc V).
BN6's other repeat prize, BugFrags, is not staked.
