# Where it is played

* [The overworld](overworld.md) - BN6's real world as stored (groups 0x00-0x06, 256-colour tiles, layers reversed) and the town `src/world/town.c` builds: Central Town as home, cut styles from copied and tiled pieces, Seaside and Green Town whole, the jack-in.

# Subdirectories

* [Home](home/) - Home in a run: Central Town and Lan's HP, the warp zone where the run's portals grow; its design, the owner's decisions, going back, jobs, the shop, a town that remembers, the Square's gate.
* [Level design](level-design/) - How BN6's own net areas are built, measured from the ROM, and how the layer generator (`src/net/`) follows them: method, the originals, navigation, identity, arrivals, props, set pieces and how they are taught.
