# Level design

* [Level design](overview.md) - How BN6's own net areas are built, measured from the ROM, and how the layer generator (`src/net/`) follows them: method, the originals, navigation, identity, arrivals, props, set pieces and how they are taught.
* [Method](method.md) - How BN6's internet maps (groups 0x90-0x96) were decoded from the ROM and measured, with a table of each map's panels, box, one-wide share, dead ends, loops, bridges and platforms.
* [Rush's gaps](rush-gaps.md) - Rush's gaps (issue #14): BN6's bone panels over a void gap to a pad of its own holding one blue Mystery Data, crossed with RushFood the Net Dealer sells (`ng_plan_gap`, `ng_carve_gap`, `rush.c`).
* [Link Navi obstacles](link-navi-obstacles.md) - Link Navi obstacles (issue #42): BN6's geysers, cybertrees, flames, cyclones and clouds in a pocket's mouth, each area's own kind, opened by the run's Crosses (`layer_block_kind`, `ng_plan_obstacle`, `blockers.c`).
* [Invisible paths](invisible-paths.md) - Invisible paths (issue #46): floor drawn as void from a walkway's tip to a lonely pad with blue Mystery Data, as BN6 hides HPMemory and AirShoes, cued by a nearby navi (`place_hidden`, `carve_hidden`, `path_panel`).
* [The generator](generator.md) - The layer generator's rules, measured from BN6's maps: a cap on the way's one-wide walkways (`net_way.c`), each area's layouts (`net_layouts.c`), the guardian's arena, walkway joins, tile picking and the floor legalizer (`legal.c`).
* [What the originals do](originals.md) - What BN6's own net maps do: two floors per area, straight one-wide walkways meeting platforms square on, 3x3 pads on spurs, many dead ends, few loops, one landmark a map and each area's shape language.
* [Arrivals](arrivals.md) - Where MegaMan arrives on a layer and which way he faces (issue #106): BN6's 54 arrivals measured by `--atlas arrivals`, then a side per layer (`net_plan.c`) and a facing along the way (`arrival_facing`).
* [Purple data](purple-data.md) - Purple Mystery Data (issue #41): one locked data at the landmark's foot on some layers, holding a chip of the rarest tiers or the run's Spin, its Unlocker stocked by the Net Dealer (`layer_purple`, `place_purple`).
* [Arrow lanes](arrow-lanes.md) - Arrow lanes (issue #43): BN6's one-way arrow panels as a layer's quick way back from a far detour (`plan_lane`, `carve_lane`), each area's own arrow art (`props_learn_arrow`), and BN6's own ride.
* [Identity](identity.md) - Layer identity (issue #98): what repeated before, each layer's signature room drawn from its area's pool (`net_signature.c`), the way run through it and its landmark, and an act's three layers kept apart (`net_plan.c`).
* [Security cubes](security-cubes.md) - Security cubes (issue #45): BN6's cube across a pocket's mouth, opened by a P-Code one bystander knows or a zenny toll in Seaside (`layer_cube_kind`, `ng_place_teller`); no navi within three panels of any piece's A.
* [Teaching them](teaching-set-pieces.md) - Teaching set pieces (issue #48): each names itself and its key at A, the Net Dealer names keys and their locks (`dealer_keys`), L senses them (`status_words`), and the map marks every shut lock.
* [Set pieces](set-pieces.md) - Set pieces and detours (issue #49): blue Mystery Data at the detours' ends (`net_detours.c`), long detours never left empty, the layer's RegUp, and the act's plan of which pieces a layer holds (`layer_pieces` in `net_pieces.c`).
* [Teleports](teleports.md) - Teleports (issue #44): BN6's paired gem pads in Green, Sky and Central, a quick way back from a long detour or the only way to a 4x4 island with blue Mystery Data (`ng_plan_teleport`, `mapslot_teleport`).
* [The Undernet's doors](undernet-doors.md) - The Undernet's doors (issue #47): on Undernet layers a number door asking how many braziers burn, sealed by a wrong answer, or a skull door opened by BN6's WWW-ID from the Net Dealer (`layer_cube_kind`).
* [Props](props.md) - Props on a layer, cut whole from BN6's maps (`src/map/props.c`) and placed by measured rules (`net_landmarks.c`): the Net Dealer's counter, each area's landmark, rows and signs, pads' warp centrepieces, framed pads and emblems.
