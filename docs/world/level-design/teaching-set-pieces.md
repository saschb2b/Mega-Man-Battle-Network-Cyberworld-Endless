# Teaching them (issue #48, in part)

A player meets each set piece without a manual, the lock and its key said
together:

- **At the piece:** a Link Navi obstacle, a cube and the Undernet's doors
  name themselves and their key at A; an invisible path has its navi.
- **From the dealer:** he names a key on his list whose lock is on his
  layer, a box each (`dealer_keys`: the Unlocker for purple data, RushFood
  and the gap's width, the WWW-ID for skull doors). A key for deeper in
  goes unsaid: the lock names it at A, and R in his list says what it is
  (eight boxes before his list, session 76;
  [the main path's words cut](../../voice/sweep/main-path.md)).
- **From L** (`status_words`): his first words on a layer name two things
  worth a detour at most, and Mystery Data he senses behind a lock is one
  of them, with where the lock stands ("The cube's straight down,far
  off!"). He no longer lists the set pieces and services: each piece says
  itself at A, and the map's key marks the services and the shut locks
  (his lessons on them and on the map's violet marks went in the voice
  sweep, docs/voice/sweep/net.md). Never an invisible path.
- **On the map** (`map_locks`, SELECT's and the 3DS's bottom screen): every
  lock still shut, an obstacle, a cube or a door, is an Event mark as the
  gates are, a ring until its panel is seen, gone once it opens; the data
  behind it stays unmarked until seen (a playtester heard of a cube's data
  and was told its P-Code, and found the cube nowhere, session 64).

Not built yet: the first layer of a run holding a kind holding its key
too.
