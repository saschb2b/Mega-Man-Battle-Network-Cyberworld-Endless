<h2 align="center">Two fixes, found by a player.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>A hotfix for 0.5.2:</b> a duel with ProtoMan counts once, and the BugFrag Trader wants its BugFrags.</p>

---

## 🛠️ Fixed

- **A duel with ProtoMan counts once.** Quitting after a duel and continuing met ProtoMan again on that layer, and each win counted anew in your record. A CONTINUE now finds the duel fought: ProtoMan is gone, and the official gate beside him stays open if you won. ([#20](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues/20))
- **The BugFrag Trader no longer trades for nothing.** Pressing A through "Try again?" kept giving chips after your BugFrags ran out. Now the ten BugFrags are taken before the chip is given, and without them the talk ends at "Too bad, Lan." ([#21](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues/21))

Both were found and reported by [@Model-0](https://github.com/Model-0), with the steps to see them. Thank you!

## 🧪 Honestly

- **A run saved by 0.5.2 continues where it stands.**
- **A record already raised by replayed duels stays as it is.** Only new duels count once.
- **The two pauses on a 3DS from 0.5.2's notes remain:** a few seconds while a new area's tiles are learned at the start of each act, and about half a second while each new layer's checkpoint is saved.
- Only Cybeast Gregar (USA) works for now; Falzar, other regions and the Legacy Collection don't yet.
