<h2 align="center">Every run leaves more behind. And it fits in your pocket.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>The third alpha of Cyberworld Endless:</b> an Android app and touch controls, a collection that carries over, gates and routes that open with you, and a MegaMan who only knows what he has seen.</p>

---

## 📱 On your phone

- **An Android app** for phones, tablets and Android handhelds (Android 5 and newer). Its first start asks for your ROM with Android's own file picker, checks it and keeps a copy beside your saves.
- **Touch controls:** on a touch screen the game draws a D-pad, A, B, L, R, Start and Select round the picture, in its own pixel art. A controller or a keyboard puts them away.
- **The browser on a phone:** the player fills the screen, turns with the phone, keeps it awake and installs to the home screen as an app.

## 🧬 Every run leaves more behind

MegaMan still starts every run as strong as the first time. What carries over is options:

- **Cross starts:** once HeatMan, ElecMan, SlashMan, EraseMan or ChargeMan has fallen as a guardian, a run can bring his Cross from the first battle. It is the run's only Cross.
- **The Library carries over:** every chip MegaMan holds joins BN6's own Library, which every run's PET shows whole. A Chip Trader's prize is new to it first, and STD, MEGA and GIGA COMP go on the title for a class complete.
- **Programs and Spins:** the NaviCust programs MegaMan has run with come back at later runs' program vendors. And each run hides one of BN6's Spins, a colour you don't have yet, in a blue Mystery Data deeper in: found, that colour's programs turn with L and R in every run after.
- **Threat 6 to 10:** five more rungs, the last with a second guardian below the Nest, each opened by a win on the one below.
- **Four towns:** a run begins in Central, ACDC, Seaside or Green Town, each with its own jack-in spot and words.

## 🗺️ A net that opens with you

- **Routes:** after each guardian, his Guardian Data asks which way on: two areas for the next act.
- **Navi gates:** from the third act a gate may stand sealed with a guardian's code. Delete that Navi twice as a guardian, in any runs, and it opens for good, to a fight with his SP.
- **Collector's vaults:** a vault that counts your Library opens on three rare chips to pick from.
- **The dark way:** once the Secret Area has been cleared, the short net's third act can go down into the Undernet.
- **Battlefields as BN6 set them:** rocks, rock and ice cubes and metal cubes are back, and now and then a green Mystery Data sits on the enemies' side. Still there when you win, it pays a second reward.
- **Rewards that fit your folder:** dealers, the gift, traders, Mystery Data and battles lean to the codes your folder holds most, and a Net Dealer never picks a chip your folder is already full of.

## 🔎 MegaMan knows what he has seen

- **A first meeting is a discovery.** A guardian MegaMan has never battled is "???" until his arena: MegaMan senses a strong Navi's signal and doesn't recognize it. The Navis on the net know more: a bystander may pass on a rumor, and from an act's second layer the Net Dealer passes on the net's word.
- **Battle data from then on.** Once battled, in any run, a guardian is named, briefed from battle data, and MegaMan reminds Lan when to strike just before the fight.
- **The PET is Lan's own.** Save saves the run where you stand. E-Mail holds Dad's mails: the dive's report, your records against every guardian, and the battle data on each one you've met. KeyItem shows the codes you hold, the Library's card and your Spins; PLACE names the area and the layer.

## 🛠️ Smoother runs

- **A won run ends properly:** words on the map as the last exit opens, and BN6's staff roll theme over the summary.
- **Saved when it matters:** as a guardian's Guardian Data appears and whenever you choose Save; "Run saved" shows in the corner, and the quit prompt says where.
- **Easier to find your way:** L's arrow holds steady, exit pads take you from anywhere on their ring, and before a guardian's arena MegaMan names the dealer and the heal on the way.
- **Safer choices:** a strong virus signal, a Navi gate or a warp into the Undernet starts on No.
- **The Custom screen:** an L or R pressed before the gauge fills is kept through a dodge.

## 📥 Get it

On [the project page](https://saschb2b.github.io/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/#play) the downloads open on your system, with the best build first.

| Where you play | Download | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In the browser** | nothing: [play here](https://saschb2b.github.io/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/play/) | Choose your ROM; runs and saves stay in your browser |
| **Windows** | `cyberworld-endless-setup-x64.exe` or `cyberworld-endless-windows-x64.zip` | Run the installer, or unpack the zip and start `cyberworld-endless.exe`. If Windows says it protected your PC: **More info**, **Run anyway** |
| **macOS** | `cyberworld-endless-macos.dmg` | Drag the app into Applications. On the first start, **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway** (it is not notarized) |
| **Android** | `cyberworld-endless.apk` | Open it on your phone and allow the install when Android asks; the app asks once for your ROM |
| **Steam Deck** | `cyberworld-endless.flatpak` | Open it in Desktop Mode (Discover installs it), then add it to Steam with its art: one line in Konsole, [in the README](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless#on-a-steam-deck) |
| **Linux PC** | `cyberworld-endless-x86_64.AppImage`, `cyberworld-endless_amd64.deb`, `cyberworld-endless.flatpak` or `cyberworld-endless-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | Make the AppImage executable and start it, or install the `.deb` or the Flatpak from your software centre, or unpack the archive anywhere |
| **ROCKNIX handhelds** (PortMaster) | `cyberworld.zip` | Unzip into `ports/`, put your ROM in `ports/cyberworld/rom/`, refresh the game list |

**You need your own ROM:** *Mega Man Battle Network 6: Cybeast Gregar (USA)*, unmodified. On first start the game finds it by its contents (in Downloads, EmuDeck's or RetroDECK's folders, or wherever you point it). Nothing in these downloads contains game data. Step-by-step guides are in the [README](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless#install).

## 🧪 An alpha, honestly

- **A run saved by 0.2.0 continues,** but its current layer starts afresh: layers are made differently now. Your profile, best depth and your guardians' memory of you stay.
- **The Spins start from none:** 0.2.0 gave every run all six. Now each run can find one.
- **Android is new:** installed and played in Android's emulator by our checks, not yet on many real phones. Tell us how it runs on yours.
- Windows and macOS are built and started by our checks but still played by few; balance is still moving session by session.
- Only Cybeast Gregar (USA) works for now; Falzar, other regions and the Legacy Collection don't yet.

Found a bug, a dead end or a fight that felt unfair? [Open an issue](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues). Everything that changed since 0.2.0 is in the [changelog](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/blob/v0.3.0/CHANGELOG.md).
