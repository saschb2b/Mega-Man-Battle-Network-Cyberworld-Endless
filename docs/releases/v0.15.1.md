<h2 align="center">The team joins the dark.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>A point release for 0.15:</b> the Navis whose Souls a run holds now liberate beside MegaMan, the dark boards play as BN5's Liberation Missions piece by piece, a dark layer's secret opens a third way at the fork, and a fast forward button speeds up everything between fights and never the fights themselves.</p>

## ⏩ Fast forward, outside the fight

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/27e6924e100bf24e8f05c1a89fcc03dd338c6460/prs/fast-forward/fast-forward.gif" width="486" alt="The same stretch of play side by side, BN6's speed on the left and fast forward on the right: the walk and a battle's opening run ahead with two orange arrows in the corner, then the fight goes at BN6's pace and the arrows go"></p>

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/27e6924e100bf24e8f05c1a89fcc03dd338c6460/prs/fast-forward/options-controls.png" width="800" alt="Options with the new Fast forward line at 3x and then 4x, its note: FAST's button speeds up walks, talk and fades. Every fight stays at BN6's pace; and the controls screen with the FAST row on Tab and Space"></p>

A press of its button runs the game faster: walking a layer and the town, chats, fades and warps, a battle's opening and its results. Held, it runs only while held. From a battle's first Custom screen to its last deletion it is BN6's speed, always, and so are BN6's menus and online play: nothing you decide against the clock changes, and there the button does nothing and the touch controls' ▶▶ steps aside. Two of the PET's orange arrows show in the corner while it runs.

- **Its button:** Tab or Space, a controller's right trigger or right stick press, ▶▶ on the touch controls. The controls screen has a **FAST** row for it. A controller's right trigger was R as well before; a `pad.ini` from before keeps it so.
- **Its speed:** Options' new **Fast forward** line sets 2x, 3x (the default), 4x or off. The music keeps its pitch, and a machine that cannot go that fast goes as fast as it can.
- **The run's minutes** count the game's own time now, so the week's DeepNet ranks a dive alike with fast forward or without.

Thanks to the player who asked for turbo because the game felt slow: the slow part was the time between decisions, not the battles.

## 🌑 Liberation Missions with a team

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/b23dd288a52d8c9f52fbefc4a468a226ca765983/prs/liberation-team/commands.png" width="800" alt="Two dark boards: ShadowMan's turn with BN5's command window, Liberation, SneakAttack and Pass, over 3 TURNS: FREES 1; and a teammate on a board of layer 11"></p>

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/b23dd288a52d8c9f52fbefc4a468a226ca765983/prs/liberation-team/soul.png" width="400" alt="A teammate's battle: MegaMan in ShadowMan's Soul on a BN5 field, TURN 1 START!"></p>

On a dark layer, the Navis whose BN5 Souls the run holds now join MegaMan on the board, as many as BN5's team of that stage holds: two at act 2, three at act 3, four at act 4. Each stands in his own BN5 body with BN5's HP for him.

- **A turn each, as BN5's team takes them.** Play passes on after each Navi acts, R hands it to the next yet to act, and those who have acted stand darker.
- **Each brings his own command** for an Order Point: Colonel's ScrenDiv frees three across, TomahawkMan's TomahawkSwing two deep and three wide, NumberMan's NumberCheck takes the items of the dark ahead with no battle, ShadowMan's SneakAttack weakens a guardian, ToadMan's LifeMelody lets a teammate beside him free five in a line, and KnightMan's StoneBody keeps harm off him and whoever stands beside him.
- **A teammate's battle** is MegaMan in that Navi's Soul from the first turn, on that Navi's HP. Deleted there, he leaves the board and the run goes on.
- The guardians and the Darkloid go for the nearest Navi. The boards are made for their team, as thick with dark as BN5's own of that stage, and a Pass now gives back a fifth of a Navi's HP, as BN5's does. A CONTINUE goes on with the same team where it stood.
