<h2 align="center">The first beta. A map on the 3DS's second screen, and touch controls for every thumb.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>The first beta of Cyberworld Endless:</b> the game is whole, and what changes from here is mostly balance and polish. The New 3DS keeps the layer's map open on its bottom screen, the touch controls are remade for thumbs, the PortMaster port reaches more handhelds, and ProtoMan's official gate is his duel's prize.</p>

---

## 🗺️ Two screens on a New 3DS

- **The map stays open on the bottom screen:** the floor MegaMan has seen, the way on to the exit or the guardian, the services and gates he has come near, and a mark on the frame's edge for those he senses. SELECT still opens it over the picture.

## 👆 Touch controls, remade

- **Sized for a thumb on every phone:** drawn at the screen's own resolution as glass plates in the PET's colours, the D-pad 3 cm across, A and B 1.4 cm. A pressed button glows, and ticks under the thumb on Android and in browsers that can.
- **Diagonals where MegaMan walks,** whose walkways run along them, and four directions in battles and menus.
- **Your layout:** MENU sets their size, opacity and haptics, and EDIT LAYOUT moves, pinches or resizes each button, or lays them out by a preset (left-handed, compact, large). Upright and sideways keep a layout each.

## ⚔️ ProtoMan and the gates

- **His official gate is the duel's prize:** it opens to whoever wins the duel beside it, and Chaud's call names what the win opens. An official Chip Order offers the Library's uncommon and rare chips first.
- **Easy to find:** the gate stands by him, the map marks him in pink with a white eye, and L says which way he waits.
- **Races you can hurry:** a squad with a Quaker, out of reach in the air, no longer turns up in a duel. Chaud's call comes once, and Lan answers him after a win too.

## 🎮 More handhelds, clearer downloads

- **The PortMaster port asks for glibc 2.29 at most** (it asked for ROCKNIX's 2.38), so ArkOS, AmberELEC, muOS and Knulli can run it, and its zip is laid out as PortMaster's own.
- **The downloads have a page of their own,** [/download/](https://saschb2b.github.io/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/download/), and every file says which system it is for.

## 🛠️ Fixes

- **A guardian's reward is taken once:** B at its program choice had left it there to take again ([#16](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues/16)).
- **HackJack's chips are gone:** a leftover of the Japanese version, one made MegaMan vanish until he was deleted ([#15](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues/15)).
- **A bought-out shop says "Sold out!"** instead of opening an empty list, and the NaviCust vendor brings his whole list ([#17](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues/17)). CONTINUE keeps what you bought.
- A strong virus signal names its prize, and vaults and official gates show what each chip hits for.
- The Recovery Mr. Prog before a guardian's arena stands a short walk from its door.
- MegaMan says which NaviCust programs L and R turn (those of a colour whose Spin you hold), and that a RUN's "OK" can hide a bug.
- CircusMan's briefing names his tell. A town's port takes R from a step off its ring. MegaMan stands behind a Net Dealer's counter, as in BN6.

## 🧪 A beta, honestly

- **A run saved by 0.4.0 continues,** but its current layer starts afresh: layers are made differently now. Your profile, best depth and records stay.
- **The 3DS's bottom screen is new** and not yet played on a 3DS: if the net runs less smoothly there than in 0.4.0, tell us.
- **The PortMaster port is built anew** for older firmwares and not yet played on each of them: tell us how it runs on yours.
- Only Cybeast Gregar (USA) works for now; Falzar, other regions and the Legacy Collection don't yet.

Found a bug, a dead end or a fight that felt unfair? [Open an issue](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues). Everything that changed since 0.4.0 is in the [changelog](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/blob/v0.5.0/CHANGELOG.md).
