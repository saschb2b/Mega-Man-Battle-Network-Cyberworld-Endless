<h2 align="center">A guardian's fall, done right.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>A polish update for 0.11:</b> what happens after a guardian falls now plays as Battle Network plays it and says half as much, and a handful of rough spots from the last playtest are smoothed out.</p>

---

## ⚔️ After a guardian

- **He logs out as BN6's Navis do.** BN6's beam rises where he stands and his sprite plays its own log-out, a frame in his lighter colours, with BN6's sound. He used to fade away after a white flash, which BN6 never does. He logs in the same way at the start of the fight.
- **His Guardian Data turns,** BN6's Mystery Data crystal spinning where it had stood still.
- **His data says half as much.** BN6's own "MegaMan got:" boxes, MegaMan's word on a Cross, the heal, and the program choice in one box and its menu, each program's colour beside its name; nothing on what BN6's players already know. Act 2's guardian went from about eighteen boxes to ten. A BN5 guardian's Soul takes three boxes, not eight.
- **Dad's mail waits** until the data is taken, in one box, where it stopped MegaMan short of the data.
- **Home's welcome after an act is three boxes,** not five.

## 🏠 Around home

- **The title's CONTINUE says Home** for a run saved between acts, where it said "Layer 7".
- **The arrow in Lan's house leads round the furniture,** straight up past the sofa's end, then to the stairs.
- **The courier's "!!" goes once he is heard,** and he leaves out the requests L already named in the town.
- **L in the town says less,** and names AsterLand's door only when Lan isn't standing at it. The Net Dealer's talk before his list went from seven boxes to four.
- **The second screen lists every way:** the ways back share one line, so a fourth way is no longer cut off.
- **The statistics' question names the buttons** on a phone: "D-pad and A, or tap Yes or No".

A run saved by 0.11.0 or 0.11.1 carries on where it was.
