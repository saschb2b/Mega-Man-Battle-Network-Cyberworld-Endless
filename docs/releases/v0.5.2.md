<h2 align="center">Smooth on the 3DS.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>A second patch for the first beta:</b> the 3DS runs the net without stutter, and the next layer comes in two seconds instead of six.</p>

---

## 🎮 New 3DS

- **The net runs smoothly.** The bottom screen's map, SELECT's map and ProtoMan's duel clock asked the game where MegaMan stood while the next frame was already running on the 3DS's other core, and each question waited for that frame: a frame lost six times a second. They now draw from what the last frame saw. A New 3DS's frame log shows 60 frames a second on the net.
- **The next layer comes three times sooner:** two seconds after you step onto the exit pad, where the game stood still for six and felt frozen. "Building the next layer..." shows over the picture while it is made.

## ⚡ Every platform

- **Layers are made five times faster**, 60 ms on a PC instead of 300. Each layer comes out exactly as before.

## 🧪 Honestly

- **A run saved by 0.5.1 continues where it stands:** the layers are the same, only made faster.
- **Two pauses remain on a 3DS.** At the start of each act, entering a new area takes a few seconds more while its tiles are learned. After every new layer, its checkpoint takes about half a second to save to the SD card. Both are next.
- **The smooth net and the faster layers were measured on a New 3DS.** The "Building the next layer..." line was checked on a PC, not yet seen on a 3DS.
- Only Cybeast Gregar (USA) works for now; Falzar, other regions and the Legacy Collection don't yet.
