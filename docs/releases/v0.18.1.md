<h2 align="center">The PS5 app starts.</h2>

<p align="center">0.18.0's PS5 app never got past the launch: the console refused it. 0.18.1 has run on a PS5, from the home screen to the title and its music, BN6's and BN5's ROMs found.</p>

---

## 🎮 The PS5

<p align="center"><img src="../screenshots/online-square.png" width="480" alt="The Net Square: MegaMan among other players' Navis, their greetings over their heads, Trade Central, the Arena and the stage at the plaza's edges"></p>

If Prospero Store installed 0.18.0, update it there, or copy the new `cyberworld-endless-ps5.zip`'s `CWEN00001/` folder over the old one in `/data/homebrew/`. Your ROM goes in `/data/cyberworld-endless/rom/`, and the saves stay beside it.

What kept it from starting, all of it on the PS5's side: a part of the app the console's loader refused, the libraries a PS5 game may use, the memory it is given and how it reads its folders. Closing it from the system no longer reports a crash.

Played on a PS5 this time, which turned up three more:

- **Online works.** The Net Square's gate connects, and the link holds instead of dropping every few seconds.
- **SELECT is the touchpad's press**, BN6's map among it: the Create button is the PS5's own, for its screenshots.
- **Fast forward no longer starts on and stays on.** The PS5's media remote, which the console lists as a controller, had held its button down.

Nothing else changes: the other systems' builds are 0.18.0's.
