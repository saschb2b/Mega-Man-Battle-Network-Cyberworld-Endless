<h2 align="center">Jack in. Dive deeper. The net is new every run.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>The first alpha of Cyberworld Endless:</b> a roguelike built around <b>Mega Man Battle Network 6</b>, running from your own cartridge's ROM.</p>

---

## 🎮 What it is

It's BN6 itself: its battles, chips, Custom screen, PET, shops and music. Cyberworld Endless builds a fresh net around it every time you jack in, and keeps the run going as deep as MegaMan can get.

A run starts in Central Town and goes act by act. Each act is an area with its own viruses and a guardian navi waiting at the end of its third layer. Then come the Undernet, the Graveyard and the Cybeast's nest, and the net starts over, harder. When MegaMan is deleted the run is over, and the next one is a new net.

## ⚡ Why one more run

- **A new net every run.** Layers are generated in BN6's own map formats: platforms, walkways, raised rooms and stairs, lit by each area's own tiles and music.
- **Read the act card, prepare, win.** The act card names the area's guardian. The Net Dealer knows what it can't stand, and sells the chip that answers it ("Word is, SpoutMan can't stand Elec chips"). MegaMan warns you what the guardian does before you walk in.
- **Build your MegaMan.** Every guardian's Guardian Data offers three NaviCust programs, one from each of three builds (a buster that matters, seven chips a turn, a shield on B+Left), or BugFrags if you take none. The NaviCust's board grows from 4x4 to 5x5 over the run. Force a program in past the edge and it runs bugged; MegaMan tells you what that costs.
- **Real BN6 fights.** Viruses are the areas' own battles, tuned to each act. Beat HeatMan, ElecMan, SlashMan, EraseMan or ChargeMan and their Cross is yours for the rest of the run. The Cybeast's BeastOut waits deeper down.
- **Risk you choose.** A Server's strong virus signal is a harder fight for a better chip. A dark flame opens the Undernet, with tougher viruses and better Mystery Data. Carry three ScrtData to the golden gate and the Secret Area opens.
- **Never lost.** Press L and MegaMan tells you where you are, what guards the layer and which way to go, and an arrow shows the way along the floor. Hold Select for the map of where you've been.

## 📥 Get it

| Where you play | Download | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In the browser** | nothing: [play here](https://saschb2b.github.io/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/play/) | Choose your ROM; runs and saves stay in your browser |
| **Steam Deck** | `cyberworld-endless.flatpak` | Open it in Desktop Mode (Discover installs it), then **Add to Steam** from the menu entry |
| **Linux PC** | `cyberworld-endless-x86_64.AppImage`, `cyberworld-endless_amd64.deb`, `cyberworld-endless.flatpak` or `cyberworld-endless-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | Make the AppImage executable and start it, or install the `.deb` or the Flatpak from your software centre |
| **ROCKNIX handhelds** (PortMaster) | `cyberworld.zip` | Unzip into `ports/`, put your ROM in `ports/cyberworld/rom/`, refresh the game list |

**You need your own ROM:** *Mega Man Battle Network 6: Cybeast Gregar (USA)*, unmodified. On first start the game finds it by its contents (in Downloads, EmuDeck's or RetroDECK's folders, or wherever you point it). Nothing in these downloads contains game data. Step-by-step guides are in the [README](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless#install).

## 🧪 An alpha, honestly

- It's tuned session by session through a long series of playtests; balance, battle variety and the new NaviCust builds are still moving.
- A save from a later build keeps your run, but may start the layer you were on again from its start.
- Only Cybeast Gregar (USA) works for now; Falzar, other regions and the Legacy Collection don't yet.
- It's been played most on Linux and in the browser. Tell us how it runs on your handheld or Deck.

Found a bug, a dead end or a fight that felt unfair? [Open an issue](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues). Everything that changed on the way here is in the [changelog](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/blob/v0.1.0/CHANGELOG.md).
