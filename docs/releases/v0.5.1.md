<h2 align="center">A fix for the 3DS, and a net that points the way.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>A patch for the first beta:</b> the 3DS build no longer crashes in the Undernet or the Nest, its bottom-screen map is lighter to draw, the arrow leads you onto walkways, and ProtoMan's gates and the Crosses say what they do.</p>

---

## 🎮 New 3DS

- **No more crash entering the Undernet or layer 10** (the Nest, in the short net), as a player reported. Learning those areas' big maps needed more memory than a New 3DS gives the game; they now need little more than half of it, and running short no longer crashes. Every platform saves the memory.
- **The bottom screen's map is lighter to draw.** 0.5.0 drew it through a slow path and lost a frame each time, six a second on the net. It is now drawn straight into the memory the GPU shows, and that screen is redrawn only when the map changes.

## 🧭 On the net

- **The arrow leads to a walkway's mouth:** along the floor to the panel the walkway starts from, then down the walkway once MegaMan is on its line. It used to point past the mouth, into the platform's corner.
- L says how far the walk to ProtoMan is, and when the way there winds.

## ⚔️ ProtoMan

- **The official gate on the layer where ProtoMan names his netbattle opens** to Chaud's clearance. It had stayed sealed as the prize of a duel that wasn't there.
- Chaud and ProtoMan say where he waits in the net's own words ("past the next two guardians"), and Chaud what beating him adds: his full clearance and the official vaults. Lan answers him.

## 🃏 Crosses and programs

- **The setup says what each Cross gives**, above what hits it twice as hard: HeatCross "Fire chips +50, buster +1", ElecCross "Elec chips +50", SlashCross "Sword chips +50", ChargeCross "One more chip each turn".
- **EraseCross works by BN6's own rule, and now says so:** a plain (elementless) chip erases a virus whose HP has a 4 in it, and bugs a Navi, whose HP then drains. The old line, "Counters erase viruses", was wrong.
- A Guardian Data's program says at its pick whether L and R turn it.
