<h2 align="center">A rival who keeps score. And a HOME Menu of its own.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>The fourth alpha of Cyberworld Endless:</b> Chaud and the real ProtoMan race you through every act, official gates open for whoever beats him, and the game runs on a New 3DS, installed from a QR code.</p>

---

## ⚔️ A rival who keeps score

- **Chaud calls on every act's second layer:** ProtoMan is on the layer, and he has busted its viruses in a time he names. Take his duel and beat that time, by BN6's own DeleteTime. The clock runs under the Custom gauge and turns red past his time.
- **The record carries over.** Chaud and Dad's Records mail keep it across runs, ProtoMan gets faster as he loses, and from the second win the race is without taking a hit.
- **The third rung is a netbattle** with ProtoMan himself, from the third act, at the act's guardian strength. It is a real fight: if he deletes MegaMan, the run is over, and MegaMan says so before you choose.
- **Official gates:** a sealed gate stands beside every duel, and Chaud's clearance opens it. A first win opens an official Chip Order; the netbattle won, the official vault's Mega chips.

## 🎮 On a New 3DS

- **A HOME Menu title:** open FBI on your 3DS, then Remote Install, then Scan QR Code, and scan the code in the 3DS tab of the [project page](https://saschb2b.github.io/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/#play). FBI installs Cyberworld Endless with its icon, and a banner that plays the trailer's opening hits.
- **Full speed:** the GBA core runs on the New 3DS's third core at 60 frames a second. The picture fills the top screen's height, or shows sharp at 1x.
- **Your ROM** goes in `sdmc:/3ds/cyberworld-endless/rom/`, or stays in `sdmc:/roms/gba/`: any file name works. A `.3dsx` for the Homebrew Launcher comes too.

## 🛠️ Smoother runs

- **Smooth motion** for 90, 144 and 165 Hz screens, a Steam Deck OLED's too: each refresh mixes the two latest frames, so motion is even.
- **Less input lag on Windows** (Direct3D 11), and sound a frame and a half sooner on desktops.
- **Windows keeps your saves.** Every save after a file's first stayed beside it as a `.tmp`; checkpoints, unlocks and records now stick.
- **MegaMan walks as in BN6:** the walking assist that could step him backward is gone.
- **Words on time:** a new area's card and MegaMan's words about it play as one moment, with MegaMan held, and A ends the card early. A NaviCust bug is named as the PET closes.
- **Safer shopping:** the first "Are you sure?" after the Net Dealer's words starts on No.
- **The BugFrag Trader trades** instead of hanging, and a kept R no longer opens a chip's description on the Custom screen.
- **Faster layers:** they are made in about a quarter less time, and a slower machine or a 50 Hz display keeps the game's pace.

## 📥 Get it

On [the project page](https://saschb2b.github.io/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/#play) the downloads open on your system, with the best build first.

| Where you play | Download | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In the browser** | nothing: [play here](https://saschb2b.github.io/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/play/) | Choose your ROM; runs and saves stay in your browser |
| **Windows** | `cyberworld-endless-setup-x64.exe` or `cyberworld-endless-windows-x64.zip` | Run the installer, or unpack the zip and start `cyberworld-endless.exe`. If Windows says it protected your PC: **More info**, **Run anyway** |
| **macOS** | `cyberworld-endless-macos.dmg` | Drag the app into Applications. On the first start, **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway** (it is not notarized) |
| **Android** | `cyberworld-endless.apk` | Open it on your phone and allow the install when Android asks; the app asks once for your ROM |
| **New 3DS** | `cyberworld-endless.cia` or `cyberworld-endless.3dsx` | Scan the project page's QR code with FBI (Remote Install, Scan QR Code), or install the CIA from the SD card with FBI. Put your ROM in `sdmc:/3ds/cyberworld-endless/rom/` |
| **Steam Deck** | `cyberworld-endless.flatpak` | Open it in Desktop Mode (Discover installs it), then add it to Steam with its art: one line in Konsole, [in the README](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless#on-a-steam-deck) |
| **Linux PC** | `cyberworld-endless-x86_64.AppImage`, `cyberworld-endless_amd64.deb`, `cyberworld-endless.flatpak` or `cyberworld-endless-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | Make the AppImage executable and start it, or install the `.deb` or the Flatpak from your software centre, or unpack the archive anywhere |
| **ROCKNIX handhelds** (PortMaster) | `cyberworld.zip` | Unzip into `ports/`, put your ROM in `ports/cyberworld/rom/`, refresh the game list |

**You need your own ROM:** *Mega Man Battle Network 6: Cybeast Gregar (USA)*, unmodified. On first start the game finds it by its contents (in Downloads, EmuDeck's or RetroDECK's folders, where 3DS players keep GBA games, or wherever you point it). Nothing in these downloads contains game data. Step-by-step guides are in the [README](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless#install).

## 🧪 An alpha, honestly

- **A run saved by 0.3.0 continues,** but its current layer starts afresh: layers are made differently now, with duels and gates on them. Your profile, best depth and records stay.
- **The 3DS build is new:** played on one New 3DS so far. The first NEW GAME boots BN6 once (about 15 seconds of black screen), and a new layer takes up to 20 seconds to make there. The older 3DS and 2DS are too slow for it.
- **ProtoMan's times are still being tuned:** a squad that spends its time in the air can make a race harder than it looks.
- Only Cybeast Gregar (USA) works for now; Falzar, other regions and the Legacy Collection don't yet.
