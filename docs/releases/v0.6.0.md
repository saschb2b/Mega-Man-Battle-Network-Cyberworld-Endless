<h2 align="center">Battle Network 5 joins in, on a net drawn like the original's.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>The second beta:</b> with Team Colonel beside your BN6 ROM, three of BN5's net areas turn up in runs, a first slice of what it can lend. Every area's layers are now drawn the way the original maps are, with whole fields, straight edges, clean stairs, and arenas paved in the area's own floor. The picture also fills a 640x480 screen.</p>

---

## 🌐 Battle Network 5 joins in

- **Three of BN5's areas in a run.** Put *Mega Man Battle Network 5: Team Colonel (USA)*, unmodified, in the same folder as your BN6 ROM. ACDC Area then stands in for Central Area, End Area for Seaside Area and Nebula Area for the Graveyard, each in about half the runs that come there.
- **In BN5's own tiles, music and faces:** its net themes (Nebula Area plays its Undernet's), its virus battle and boss themes, its battle backgrounds behind the fights, and its purple HeelNavis as bystanders. The layouts, guardians and viruses stay the BN6 areas'.
- **Optional, always:** BN6 alone plays as before. When the game has found BN5, a note in the title's top right corner says "BN5 found" for a few seconds as it starts. It works on Linux, macOS, Windows, the Steam Deck and PortMaster handhelds, and the desktop builds keep BN5's ROM beside their copy of BN6's, so its areas stay after the first start.

Each picture shows one spot of one layer twice: as 0.5.3 drew it on the left, as 0.6.0 draws it on the right.

**Guardian arenas are paved in their area's floor:** Robot Control's white slab, Mr. Weather Comp's solar panels, Central's green field and Green's grass, where a jumble of walkway tiles had been.

- **Fields are laid as the originals lay them:** by their repeating pattern, or a whole stretch of the original field repeated. Mr. Weather Comp's solar panels keep their lights and the Cybeast Nest's floors their cracks, with no fan belt or half dome cut off in a field.
- **Stairs meet their floors cleanly** and lead up onto their raised rooms. MegaMan no longer drops under a room at a stair's top and walks out of bounds ([#22](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues/22)).

## 🖥️ The whole screen at 640x480

- **The picture fills a 640x480 screen,** such as an RG35XX Pro's or Plus's: 640x427 where it had been 480x320, each pixel's edge a little soft. Screens a whole scale fits well, like the Nova's and the Flip 2's, look as before. `screen = whole` in `settings.ini` keeps whole pixels everywhere, and `screen = fill` fills every screen ([#36](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues/36)).

## ⚔️ Fights you can read

- **CircusMan's tent marks the panel it drops on,** in warning yellow over MegaMan, for the half second when a step can still clear it.
- **ProtoMan's netbattle can be won:** he has half the act's guardian band (500 HP in the third act, where he had 1000), and MegaMan says how he fights as the netbattle is offered.
- **The tells are said before the fight:** Colonel's two moves, SpoutMan's whirl reaching the panels at his corners, EraseCross's drain on a Navi, StarFish bubbles that soak up shots, and that there's no running from a guardian.
- **No random battle under a guardian's staging,** fought between its black bars ([#24](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues/24)).

## 🧭 Clearer on the way

- **The arrow leads a hurt MegaMan to the Recovery Mr. Prog first.** It also points down the next walkway at a corner, turns as soon as he stops, and shows when the map closes.
- **A one-time pick says what the chip does,** in BN6's own words, and asks once more before it's yours. A Navi chip's EX and SP versions show their damage.
- **The NaviCust explains itself:** MegaMan names the program and the rule behind a bug, and says when a new program needs room made first. The Guardian Data names each program's colour.
- **MegaMan says where the golden gate stands.** The official gates name the clearance they take, and a Cross's weakness is said to break it.

## 🛠️ Fixes

- **An act continued from a save keeps its virus count** and its Net Dealer's greeting, and CONTINUE no longer greets a saved ScrtData as a new find.
- **A held B no longer hurries a talk past unseen.**
- **Bystanders don't repeat themselves across an act,** and the Net Dealer no longer denies the net's rumours.
- **The way on names a never-met guardian as "??? (Aquarium)",** not by his area alone.

## 🧪 A beta, honestly

- **A run saved by 0.5.3 continues,** but its current layer starts afresh, because layers are made differently now. Your profile, best depth and records stay.
- **BN5 is a first slice.** SciLab sits out for now: its maps hold no platform bigger than a small pad, and the rooms drawn in its tiles still look rough. More of BN5 is planned for later versions.
- **BN5's areas aren't on the 3DS** (no memory for a second ROM), on Android or in the browser yet.
- **The 640x480 picture is tested headless only.** If you play on an RG35XX or another 640x480 handheld, tell us how it looks.
- **The two pauses on a 3DS remain:** a few seconds while a new area's tiles are learned at the start of each act, and about half a second while each new layer's checkpoint is saved.
- Only Cybeast Gregar (USA) works for BN6, and Team Colonel (USA) for BN5.
