<h2 align="center">Locks that hold.</h2>

<p align="center"><b>A hotfix for 0.11:</b> security cubes, Link Navi obstacles and Rush's gaps can no longer be walked past, and the browser's player is the game alone on its screen.</p>

---

## 🛠️ Fixed

- **Cubes, obstacles and Rush's gaps hold MegaMan until they open** ([#111](https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/issues/111)). Some stood the wrong way round: MegaMan could walk past a security cube or a Link Navi obstacle without its P-Code or Cross, or across a gap without RushFood, and then could not walk back. Each now faces the side you come from, and both sides of a gap stay shut until Rush lies in it. Thanks to whitt-e for the report and the photo.

## 🌐 The browser's player

- **The game alone on its screen,** at the largest whole scale your window holds, framed in the PET's green. The site's bar steps away while you play.
- **The ROMs go in on the screen itself,** and a return visit is one gold **Play**.
- **One PET menu holds everything else:** **Menu** above the screen, or F1, pauses the game and its sound. Fullscreen, Smooth motion, the controls, Save backup, Load backup and Forget all are all there.

A run saved on a layer starts that layer again, from where MegaMan arrived; everything else carries on. A MegaMan stuck behind a cube or on a gap's far side is free.
