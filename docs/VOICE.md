# Voice

Every line a character says in Cyberworld Endless is read in BN6's own chat
box, beside BN6's own face for that character. It has to sound like BN6.
A player wrote in October 2026 that our dialogue "sometimes feels like I'm
reading a summary of a regular sentence". They were right: our boxes held too
much, said it flatly, and explained where BN6's people just talk.

This page is the measure for all of it: MegaMan, Lan, Mr.Prog, the shops,
the bystanders, the guardians, Dad, Chaud, ProtoMan, the townsfolk, the
rumors, the PET's mails and the BBS. Read it before writing or changing any
of them (AGENTS.md points here). Menus, labels, the README and the site
keep their own plain style.

It was made by reading BN6's whole English script: the 916 text files of
the bn6f disassembly (docs/SOURCES.md), about 25,500 boxes, sorted by
speaker and by situation. That script is Capcom's and stays out of this
repository: what follows describes it, and every example line on this page
is ours.

Across the script, a box ends in "!" about 2 times in 5, in "..." 1 time
in 6, in "?" 1 time in 9. Colons, semicolons and parentheses almost never
appear in anyone's mouth (inner thoughts in parentheses are a rare joke).
A comma takes no space after it, everywhere: `Lan,look!`.

## How BN6's people talk

### One beat a box

A box is one breath: a reaction, one fact, or a call to act. Six words is
normal, twelve the most. Two short sentences can share a box when they are
one beat ("Whoa! That's huge!"). More to say means another box; three
boxes is a lot for one person at once.

### Feeling comes first

BN6's people react, then inform. A box very often opens with a sound or a
word of feeling, and the fact follows in the next box or not at all.

- Surprise: "Whoa!", "Huh!?", "What!?", "No way!"
- Joy: "Alright!", "Yeah!", "We did it!!"
- Relief: "Phew...", "Whew!"
- Doubt: "Hmm...", "Um...", "...Huh?"
- Annoyance: "Geez...", "C'mon!", "Hmph."

### Questions

Short, and often bare: "Huh?", "What's that?", "Lan?". A question that
trails off ends in "...?", one asked in shock in "!?", one shouted in
"?!". Characters ask each other things instead of stating them: "Did you
hear that?" before "Something's coming."

### Fright

Fear stammers and stretches: "W-What was that!?", "N-No way...", "L-Lan!",
"Waaaah!!", "Yikes!". It cuts sentences off with "...!" and doubles the
marks. A frightened Navi may squeak a word twice: "Help! Help!".

### Jokes and fooling around

BN6 is funny, and MegaMan is the comedian as often as Lan.

- **MegaMan teases Lan like a brother:** his homework, his grades,
  oversleeping, bedtime, his appetite. He reads awful poems aloud, runs a
  quiz show, and is proud of bad jokes.
- **Lan fires back:** mock outrage ("Cut it out!!"), groans, a bad joke of
  his own, or he just laughs: "Hahaha! Not bad!!"
- **Actions go in asterisks:** \*gulp\*, \*yawn\*, \*sigh\*, \*grin\*,
  \*huff,huff\*. Use them for a moment of body language, rarely.
- **Navis are silly too:** a Mr.Prog plays a car ("VROOM! VROOM!") or a
  dog, a bystander laughs too long, a tough HeelNavi cackles.
- The humor is warm, never mean, never winking at the player.

### Banter

Lan and MegaMan talk in quick turns, one box each: MegaMan notices or
warns, Lan reacts and decides, and both cheer. A battle opens with Lan's
call and MegaMan's answer, the series' own call and response. A long
explanation from one of them reads wrong; split it between them, or cut
it.

### Resolve

"Let's go!", "Leave it to me!", "We've gotta stop him!", "Don't give up,
MegaMan!". Short, with an exclamation, naming the goal.

### Worry and sadness

"...", "I wonder...", a sentence left unfinished. Quiet lines are short
too: "...Dad..." can be a whole box.

### Teaching how something works

BN6 explains its systems, but plainly and in the speaker's voice: one fact
a box, as an instruction or a promise, never a list. "Press L to look
around." "If it hits 0, he changes back." Dad and Mr.Prog teach most; a
Mr.Prog does it in capitals. MegaMan reminds, he doesn't lecture.

### How it looks

- A comma has no space after it: `Lan,let's go!`
- `Mr.Prog`, `Zenny`, `NetBattle`, `NaviCust`, `BugFrag`, `HPMemory`,
  `Pack`, `Folder`, `PET` are written as BN6 writes them.
- "!!" for big moments, "!?" for shocked questions, "..." for pauses,
  trails and silence, stretched vowels for shouts ("Laaaan!").
- Stutters with a hyphen: "W-Wait!", "I-I'm fine..."
- Mr.Prog speaks in ALL CAPITALS.

## The cast

Each entry: who they are, how they talk, and lines of ours in their voice.

### MegaMan

Lan's Navi and brother: earnest, warm, brave, a little careful, and a big
tease. He says "Lan" constantly, usually first ("Lan,look!"), and "we" and
"let's". He warns ("Watch out!"), cheers ("We can do it!"), reports what
he sees in one box, and nags about homework and sleep.

> Lan,look! A Mystery Data!
> Whoa... This place is huge.
> Lan,are you even listening? \*sigh\*
> Phew... That was close!
> W-Wait! Something's here!
> Hehe. You're hungry again,aren't you?

### Lan

A sixth grader: energetic, impulsive, cheerful, easily scared for a
moment, never for long. Short bursts, lots of "Alright!", "Let's go,
MegaMan!", "Huh?", "No way!". He decides fast, groans at homework and
laughs at himself.

> Alright! Let's go,MegaMan!
> Huh? What's that over there?
> N-No way...!
> Leave it to us!
> Ugh... I'm starving.
> Hahaha! Okay,okay!

### Mr.Prog

The net's helper programs: ALL CAPITALS, cheerful, a bit dramatic, simple
words, now and then a word said twice. They greet, explain one thing, and
fuss.

> HELLO! WELCOME,WELCOME!
> I'LL PATCH YOU UP! HOLD STILL!
> OH NO,OH NO! VIRUSES EVERYWHERE!
> THAT'S ALL I CAN DO TODAY!

### The Net Dealer and shop Navis

Friendly sellers: "Welcome!", "Take a look!", "Come again!". One short
line about what's special, no explaining.

> Welcome! Need chips?
> Thanks! Come back anytime!
> Sorry,that's sold out!

### Bystander Navis

Normal Navis chatter, gossip and complain: "Did you hear?", "This area's
tough...", "Hey,hey!". HeelNavis talk tough and cackle ("Heh heh heh...").
GirlNavis gush ("Sooo cute!"). A tip is told as gossip, never as a manual.

> Did you hear? There's a shop near every guardian!
> Ugh,these viruses are way too strong...
> Heh heh... You lost,kid?

### Townsfolk

Ordinary people: kids, parents, shopkeepers, old folks. Small talk, a bit
of humor, a rumor.

> My mom says I play too many NetBattles.
> Back in my day,the net was just one big room!

### Dad

Kind, calm, proud of Lan and MegaMan. He explains in plain steps, a little
longer than the others (7 words a box), and fusses like a father: "Now,
now.", "Be careful,OK?".

> MegaMan,Lan... Be careful down there.
> I'll keep a backup of MegaMan,just in case.
> Now,now. Don't look so down.

### Chaud

An official NetBattler: cool, terse, competitive, secretly respects Lan.
Few exclamations, many "Hmph." and "...", orders and challenges.

> Hmph. Still in one piece,Lan?
> Don't get careless.
> ...Not bad.

### ProtoMan

Chaud's Navi: a stern swordsman of few words. "Roger." "Leave this to me."

> ...You're slow,MegaMan.
> Leave this to me.

### The guardians

Proud, theatrical, each with his own tic. A taunt as he meets MegaMan, a
groan or a grudging word as he falls: one or two boxes each, never more.

BN5's Navis (KnightMan, ShadowMan, NumberMan, ToadMan, TomahawkMan's BN5

role, not from a measured script: BN5's isn't read here.

A Navi's tic goes in about every other box, not every one.

## Our recurring lines

How each kind of line in this game should go. The facts stay; the voice
changes.

| Line | Who | Too much (avoid) | Right |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arriving on a layer | MegaMan | "We're on Central Area's layer 3. A guardian waits at its end, and a Mr.Prog stands to the north." | "Lan,we're in!" / "A guardian's waiting up ahead!" |
| L, the way on | MegaMan | "The exit lies down and to the left, but the way winds: follow the arrow." | "The exit's down-left!" / "It winds,though. Follow the arrow!" |
| A shop | Net Dealer | "Welcome to the Net Dealer! Divers need chips, and I've got 'em, in your folder's codes when I can!" | "Welcome! Chips for sale!" / "I've got your codes,too!" |

| A gift or reward | MegaMan | "It went to our Pack. We can put it in the folder through the PET." | "Got it! It's in our Pack." / "Let's add it in the Folder!" |

## What makes a line read "written by a machine"

Check for these before committing a line:

- **The summary voice:** a sentence that states several facts at once,
  joined by colons, semicolons, dashes or "which/whose/where".
- **Formal glue words:** "however", "additionally", "therefore", "in
  order to", "currently". BN6 says "But", "So", "And".
- **Explaining the screen:** telling the player what they can see.
- **Rule talk:** "this layer", "this run", "the player", numbers that
  read like a stat sheet. Characters speak in their world: "down here",
  "this time", "our PET", "the net".
- **Every box the same shape:** vary openers; let some boxes be a single
  word or a sound.
- **No feeling at all:** a box with no reaction, no warmth, no humor,
  where BN6 would put one.
- **Hedging and politeness padding:** "it seems that", "you might want
  to". BN6's people say it straight.

## Before you commit a line

1. Read it aloud as the character. If it sounds like a manual or a news
   report, rewrite it.
2. Count the words in each box: around six, never past twelve.
3. Does it need to be said at all? If the screen or a menu already says
   it, cut it.
4. Look at it in the game (`--talk`, docs/DEVTOOLS.md): BN6's box is three
   lines of about twenty characters. A box that pages on for one word is
   too long.
