# Running BN6

* [The GBA's screen: colour correction, frame blending, lcd3x](lcd.md) - Options' Display can show the game as a GBA's own screen did (issue #265): mGBA's libretro core's Color Correction and Interframe Blending on BN6's picture, step for step (`lcd_frame.c`), libretro's handheld colour shaders (VBA, DS, PSP) over that picture as RetroArch runs them, the chosen colours on what the engine draws over it too (`lcd_canvas.c`), and RetroArch's lcd3x shader over the screen (`lcd_grid.c`); what each does, where it runs, and how it was checked against mGBA's own code and the shaders' math.
* [Depth lighting: the world lit in 3D](voxel.md) - Options' Display's Depth lighting (off by default, `depth_lighting` in settings.ini; the code's `voxel_` files, its first name) lights the world as the game runs, on the net, in town and in battle (a sun with soft shadows, shaded sides, a glow, what is far out of focus), and on a net layer rebuilds the picture as columns under a camera a little lower than BN6's: how a frame is read, lifted over the layer's own floor, lit and drawn at the screen's scale, where it runs, what it leaves alone, and what is still open.
* [The sound out: levels, output and filters](sound-out.md) - Options' Audio sets how the game is heard on the player's speakers, handheld or headphones: Music and Effects in ten steps of 3 dB (`sound_heard.c`), Output as Speakers, Headphones (bs2b's crossfeed, `crossfeed.c`) or Mono, and Sound as GBA, Soft or mGBA's libretro Audio Filter step for step (`sound_filter.c`); what each does, where it runs, what a NetBattle keeps alike, and how each was checked against its source's own code and measured.
* [Portable saves](saves.md) - Portable saves (issue #114): the SAVES screen's three views (`src/saves/`), the `.cwsave` carrier and manifest (`src/core/backup*.c`), what travels, import checks, UNDO IMPORT and recovery, and the automatic copy (`mirror.c`).
* [Settings screens elsewhere: what Options can take](settings-screens.md) - How well-regarded games, the accessibility guidelines, emulator frontends and retro collections show a setting's effect while it is chosen (live preview, a still frame behind the menu, a picture per setting, a one-press look), describe, confirm, reset and lay out their settings, researched on 2 November 2026 for Options' Display, what of it fits a PET panel at 240x160, and what Options was rebuilt with: pages on L and R, START's defaults, and BN6's own battle looping beside Display's lines.
* [The game's own updates](updates.md) - How the game learns a newer version is out and, where it can, puts it in place itself: the release's update.json, the look at each start (on by default, Options' Updates line switches it off), the title's note and Options' update line, each system's way (the AppImage over itself, the Windows installer run silently, the PortMaster launcher after the game, the download page, the itch app), the checks before a file is placed, and what other systems are to get.
* [The PET's screens: Battle Network's rules](ui.md) - BN6's PET screens' rules measured from Gregar (band, windows, information box, cursor, motion, keys, letters, colours) and what the engine's own screens (the Jukebox, Options and its pages and scene, Trade Central) take from them through `pet_look.c`.
