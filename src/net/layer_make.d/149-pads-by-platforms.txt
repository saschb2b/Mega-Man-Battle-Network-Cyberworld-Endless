A pad's stamp set flush against a platform shares the tile with it on the two tile layers (paste_pad): a platform behind the pad under the stamp, one in front of it picked again with the pad taken for void (pick_beside_pads) and over the stamp, hiding the pad's side faces; a tile under a platform's face beside a pad counts as by it. Robot Control's and Sky's layers draw other tiles where a pad touches a platform.
