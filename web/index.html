A roguelike on Mega Man Battle Network 6. Every run builds a new net in the style of BN6's areas, and the game runs it: its battles, chips, Navis and music. Go as deep as you can. You need your own ROM.

The run

Acts 6+1
1 · Act

An act is three layers in one area. Its card names the guardian at the end.

2 · Layer

Each layer is a new map, laid out after the area's own. Mystery Data, shops, and a pad down to the next layer.

3 · Guardian

The third layer ends in an arena. Delete its Navi to go on to the next area.

Navigator

After six acts comes the Cybeast Nest at layer 19. Then the cycle starts again, harder. The run is saved on every new layer. If MegaMan is deleted, the run is over.

Battle

V1 to SP
Navigator

Random battles use BN6's virus formations from the area's maps.

Depth sets how strong they are. Each battle's virus HP and hardest hit are checked against the HP MegaMan should have by then, and the viruses come at the highest version that fits.

The first battles of a run, and those after a guardian, are easier. A Server offers a harder battle for a better chip.

Navis

Guardians 17
Navigator

Each area has its guardians. BlastMan, DiveMan or SpoutMan come first; Colonel appears from the fourth act. A guardian remembers whether it won or lost the last time you met.

Deleting one gives five HPMemory, its Navi chip and a full heal. HeatMan, ElecMan, SlashMan, EraseMan and ChargeMan also give their Cross.
