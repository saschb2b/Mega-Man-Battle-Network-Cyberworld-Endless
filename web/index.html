A roguelike on Mega Man Battle Network 6

Every run builds a new net in the style of BN6's areas, and the game itself runs it.

Jack in Download

One run

About an hour, ten layers down
  1. Lan's room

    Jack in

    A run begins at home. Lan's PC jacks MegaMan in to the Endless Net, whose paths change every time.

  2. Layer 1

    A new net every run

    Three layers an act, each in the style of one of BN6's areas: its viruses, Mystery Data, Net Dealers and Mr.Progs.

  3. Every layer

    BN6's own battles

    Its random battles, chips and Custom screen, run by the game itself. They grow with the depth, not the area.

  4. Off the way

    BN6's set pieces

    Rush's gaps, Link Navi obstacles a Cross clears, security cubes and teleports, each guarding data.

  5. Layer 3

    A guardian ends each act

    A copied Navi in his arena; the Net Dealer stocks a chip that answers him. His data: HP, his chip, his Cross.

  6. Between acts

    Home between acts

    Central Town remembers the run. AsterLand orders any chip the Library holds, and three people post a request.

  7. Layer 10

    The Nest

    After three acts, the Cybeast Nest. Its guardian's fall wins the run.

The run is won. And it opens what lies below.

The endless net

Nineteen layers through the Undernet and the Graveyard to the Cybeast's den, then around again, harder. Bass waits in the Secret Area.

Battle Network 5, too

Put Team Colonel (USA) beside BN6, and BN5's areas turn up in runs, their battles fought in BN5's own engine.

Runs leave options

Not power: every run starts as strong as the first. A folder, a Cross, ten threat rungs, and Dad keeps your record.
