Which ROM do I need?

Mega Man Battle Network 6: Cybeast Gregar (USA), unmodified, as the .gba file dumped from your own cartridge. Its SHA-1 is 89fe0bac4fd3d2ab1d2ca35e87ef8b1294a84cd6. The file's name doesn't matter: the game checks what is in it.

Cybeast Falzar, other regions and the Legacy Collection's version don't work yet.

Can Battle Network 5 join in?

Yes, if you have it: put Mega Man Battle Network 5: Team Colonel (USA), unmodified, in the same folder as your BN6 ROM (SHA-1 5f472f78d8de2df01d5039e045c043cb40969a39). Six of BN5's net areas then turn up in runs in place of the BN6 areas they resemble, each in about half the runs that come there: ACDC Area for Central Area, Oran Area for Green Area, SciLab for Sky Area, End Area for Seaside Area, BN5's Undernet for the Undernet and Nebula Area for the Graveyard, in BN5's own tiles, music, battle backgrounds and bystanders. Their random battles are BN5's own, fought in BN5's engine; their guardians stay BN6's.

When the game has found it, a note in the title's top right corner says BN5 found as the game starts. BN6 alone plays as before. It works on Linux, macOS, Windows, the Steam Deck, PortMaster handhelds, Android, iPhones and iPads: there, choose the folder that holds both ROMs at the first start, or put BN5 in it later. On a PC, a Mac, a phone or a tablet, R on the title also opens the ROMs screen, where BN5's cartridge slot takes it. In the browser, choose it on the player's page beside BN6, or add it later; the first time, BN5 starts up while the title shows. Not on the 3DS.

Why isn't the game included?

Because the ROM is the game. BN6's battles, chips, viruses, PET, shops and music all run from it on an embedded mGBA core; Cyberworld Endless builds the net around them and keeps the run going. No download and no page holds Capcom's data, so without the ROM there is no game.

It is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom.

Does the browser upload my ROM?

No. The player checks each file, BN6's and BN5's if you add it, and keeps them, with your saves, in the browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on your device. The next visit starts straight away, and Forget all in its menu removes them.

Does the game send anything?

Only if you say yes. At its first start the game asks once whether it may send anonymous play statistics, the cursor on No, and sends nothing before a yes. A yes sends the system and version it runs on and the screen's size as it starts, each run's setup as it begins, how each guardian's battle ends (won or lost, MegaMan's HP left, its seconds) and how each run ends (how far, by whom, its minutes), to the game's own site on the developer's Umami. No names, no IDs of you or your device, no ROMs, saves or file paths, and no cookies.

To change it, Select on the title opens the controls screen, whose Statistics row shows your answer and asks again; on the 3DS, Select on the title asks again. statistics = off in settings.ini does the same. This site's pages count their own visits and downloads with Umami, anonymously and without cookies, apart from the game's.

Where can I play?

On handhelds with PortMaster (AmberELEC, ArkOS, Knulli, muOS, ROCKNIX and more; made on a Retroid Nova and a Retroid Pocket Flip 2), on a Steam Deck, on an x86-64 Linux PC (Flatpak, AppImage or .deb), on Windows 10 and 11 (an installer or a zip), on a Mac (macOS 11 on), on Android phones, tablets and handhelds (an APK), on an iPhone or iPad (iOS 14 on, through SideStore or AltStore Classic), on a New 3DS (a HOME Menu title FBI installs from a QR code, or the Homebrew Launcher), and in a browser, a phone's too.

On a touch screen the game draws its own D-pad and buttons round the picture, sized for a thumb, and a MENU button that sizes and moves them; a controller or a keyboard puts them away.

How do I add it to Steam on a Deck?

In Desktop Mode, install the Flatpak from the releases (Discover opens it), then paste this into Konsole:

python3 "$(flatpak info --show-location io.github.saschb2b.Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless)/files/share/cyberworld-endless/steam/add-to-steam.py"

Steam closes for a moment and opens again with the game in its library under Non-Steam, with its own capsule, banner and logo. In Gaming Mode, Steam hands it the Deck's controls. The AppImage offers the same on its first start.

What is a run?

A run starts in town: Central, ACDC, Seaside or Green Town. Walk to its statue or fountain and press R: MegaMan jacks in to the first layer of a net that is new every run, laid out in the style of one of BN6's areas. Find the exit pad to go a layer deeper; the area's viruses meet you on the way.

Three layers make an act, and a guardian Navi waits at the end of each. His Guardian Data asks which way on: two areas for the next act, each named with its guardian.

A run is the short net: three acts, then the Cybeast Nest on layer 10, whose fall wins the run. A win opens the endless net: acts 1 to 4 on the surface, the story's comps and the homepages, act 5 the Undernet, act 6 the Graveyard, layer 19 the Nest, where the Cybeast Gregar itself waits, then again, harder. Once the Secret Area has been cleared, Bass waits behind its golden gate.

How does MegaMan get stronger?

On the first layer a Mr. Prog offers a gift: two HPMemory, a ★3 chip or a NaviCust program.

Every guardian leaves Guardian Data: five HPMemory, its Navi chip, its Cross where it has one, and a choice of NaviCust programs. The second and fourth guardians grow the NaviCust to 5x4, then 5x5. Beating the Cybeast at the endless net's own Nest gives BeastOut for the rest of the run, and from then on the setup can bring it from the first battle.

Chips come from Mystery Data, Net Dealers and traders, rarer the deeper you go.

What happens when MegaMan is deleted?

The run is over. The title shows how deep you got, how many viruses and Navis you deleted, and your best depth, which stays. The guardians remember how your battles with them went, run after run.

If a run ends before its first guardian, the next run's gift comes with an extra HPMemory.

What carries over between runs?

MegaMan starts every run as strong as the first time: what runs leave behind is options. From the second run, NEW GAME opens a setup: the short or the endless net, a starting folder (Blade and Storm open with milestones), a Cross to bring from the first battle once its Navi has fallen as a guardian, a threat rung (each win opens the next) and helpers, BeastOut from the first battle among them once the endless net's own Nest has fallen.

Every chip MegaMan holds joins the Library, which every run's PET shows whole, and the NaviCust programs he has run with come back at later runs' program vendors. A gate sealed with a Navi's code opens for good once you have deleted him twice as a guardian, and his SP waits behind it; a collector's vault opens for a Library big enough, with three rare chips to pick from. Each run hides one of the NaviCust's Spins, a colour you don't have yet, in a blue Mystery Data deeper in: found, its colour's programs turn with L and R in every run after. Milestones put BN6's own marks on the title.

When is the run saved?

Each time MegaMan arrives on a layer and as a guardian's Guardian Data appears, whenever you choose Save in the PET, and when you quit while he is free to move (not in a battle, a talk or a guardian's scene). CONTINUE on the title brings you back there.

Do my saves survive an uninstall?

On a PC or a Mac, yes: they live in the game's data folder, which uninstalling leaves alone. On Android, an iPhone or an iPad, uninstalling deletes the app's own files. With Auto-export on, the app keeps cyberworld-endless.cwsave in the transfer folder shown in SAVES, renewed a few seconds after each save. It is on by default, using your ROM folder; Options → Choose folder can select another destination. After a reinstall, select your ROMs again and use SAVES → Import a file to choose that file. A copy in the ROM folder is also found when you choose that folder again.

SAVES on the title exports your progress and current checkpoint as one cyberworld-endless.cwsave file on every system. Import a file on the other device compares its saves with the file before replacing them; Keep this device is selected first, and Use file takes the other copy. Details pages show full device names, versions and paths. Undo last import previews the previous saves before Restore brings them back. The screen shows whether an automatic folder copy is pending, complete or failed; Options → Retry copy tries again after checking for incoming saves. A folder copy does not confirm cloud sync. Controls, display settings, volumes and your statistics answer stay on each device. In the browser, Save backup and Load backup in the player's menu open this same SAVES screen.

A newer game version, an incompatible checkpoint or a damaged file is refused without changing your saves. A run that needs BN5 requires its ROM on the receiving device too.

Can I change the keys?

The keyboard starts with the layout of Capcom's PC version: WASD move, J is A, K is B, Q and E are L and R, Enter is Start and R is Select. Keys are positions, so on AZERTY you move with Z Q S D. Controllers use their own buttons.

Select on the title screen opens the controls screen, here in the browser too. It has a tab for the controller and one for the keyboard (L and R switch them), and a row for each GBA button with a MAIN and an ALSO slot: choose one and press the button or key you want there. D-PAD asks for its four directions in turn, so a stick, a hat or keys can move MegaMan; A and B has presets, B on X (left of A, as on the GBA) among them. A button another one had swaps over, X clears a slot, Set all asks for every button in turn, for a controller laid out its own way, and DONE keeps it all once you press the new A. The screen itself always answers the controller's own A, B and D-pad, the keyboard and taps. pad.ini and keys.ini in the save folder hold them.

On a touch screen, the MENU button beside the buttons pauses the game: SIZE, OPACITY and HAPTICS for them all, CONTROLLER where one is connected, and EDIT LAYOUT, where you drag a button where your thumb wants it, pinch it or drag a corner to size it, or take a preset such as LEFT-HANDED. DONE keeps it. The phone upright and on its side keep an arrangement each.

What is BN6's own, and what is new?

BN6's own, running from your ROM: battles and viruses, chips and Busting Levels, the PET, shops and traders, the guardians' boss battles, the sprites, faces, text engine and music.

Built by the engine in the game's formats: every layer's layout, its floors (tiles learned from the area's original maps), its walls and what stands where. Written for the run: the Endless Net's story, what people say, the run summary.

How does it run BN6?

An embedded mGBA core runs the game's own code from your ROM. Cyberworld Endless is the director around it: it builds each layer in the game's map formats, points the map tables of the core's in-memory copy of the ROM at it (your file is never written), and watches the game's memory to move the run along.

Why not a native port, as Ship of Harkinian is for Ocarina of Time? BN6 has no C decompilation, its picture and sound would need emulating anyway, and a native build would ship Capcom's code. The emulator runs your ROM and ships none of it.

How do I hear about new versions?

On GitHub, open the repository and choose Watch › Custom › Releases: GitHub tells you when a new version is out. Without an account, add the release feed to any RSS reader. New versions are announced in the Discussions too, beside questions, ideas and players' runs.
