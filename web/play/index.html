The layout of Capcom's PC version. Keys are positions: on AZERTY, Z Q S D move. Select on the title screen opens the controls screen, which sets the keys and the controller's buttons. On a phone or tablet the game draws its own buttons round the picture; added to the home screen, it opens full screen.

Saves

The run is saved in this browser on every new layer; CONTINUE on the title picks it up.

Save backup, in the PET's menu, keeps your saves in one file, cyberworld-endless.cwsave, which Load backup brings back here or in another browser, and which the Android and iPhone apps take too, put in the folder your ROMs are in. Forget all, or clearing this site's data, deletes your ROMs and saves: back them up first.
