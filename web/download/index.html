    Every system

    Computer

    • Windows Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Also: portable zip
      Installer
      How to install
      1. Start the installer: it installs for you alone, no administrator, with a Start menu entry. Or unpack the zip anywhere and start cyberworld-endless.exe.
      2. If Windows says it protected your PC: More info, then Run anyway. The game isn't signed.
      3. On its first start, the game asks for your ROM.
    • Mac One app for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 and newer.
      Mac app
      How to install
      1. Open the .dmg and drag the app into Applications.
      2. It isn't notarized, so macOS stops its first start: open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
      3. The game asks for your ROM.
    • Linux Any x86-64 distribution. On a Steam Deck? Take its Flatpak. Also: Flatpak · .deb for Debian, Ubuntu and Mint · a plain folder
      AppImage
      How to install
      1. Make the AppImage executable and start it. It offers to add itself to your menu and to Steam.
      2. Or install the Flatpak with your software centre (Discover, GNOME Software), or the .deb with the system; the plain folder needs nothing installed (its install.sh adds a menu entry).
      3. On its first start, the game looks for your ROM in Downloads and in EmuDeck's and RetroDECK's folders, or asks for it.

    Phone & tablet

    • Android Android 5 and newer: phones, tablets and Android handhelds. Touch controls, or a controller.
      Android app
      How to install
      1. Open the .apk, and allow your browser or file manager to install it when Android asks.
      2. The app asks once for your ROM and keeps a copy beside your saves; uninstalling deletes both.
    • iPhone & iPad iOS 14 and newer, through SideStore, which installs it with your Apple ID and keeps it up to date. Not AltStore PAL, the EU's App Marketplace edition: it installs only apps Apple has notarized. Also: add to AltStore Classic · the IPA · no setup? play in Safari
      Add to SideStore
      How to install
      1. Set up SideStore once, with a computer: iloader (Windows, macOS or Linux) installs it over a USB cable, then the LocalDevVPN app on the phone lets SideStore install and renew apps by itself (SideStore's guide). On iOS 16 and newer, turn on Developer Mode too. AltStore Classic works the same way, with AltServer on a Windows PC or a Mac.
      2. On the iPhone, tap Add to SideStore here. Or in the app: Sources, +, and this address: https://github.com/saschb2b/Mega-Man-Battle-Network-Cyberworld-Endless/releases/latest/download/altstore-source.json
      3. Install Cyberworld Endless from the source. With a free Apple ID an app lasts 7 days: SideStore renews it, and offers each new version.
      4. Start it and tap CHOOSE ROM: pick your ROM in Files. Or put it in Files, On My iPhone › Cyberworld.

    Handheld & console

    • Steam Deck Installed in Desktop Mode, played in Gaming Mode with its own art in your library. Also: AppImage · a plain folder
      Flatpak
      How to install
      1. In Desktop Mode, open the Flatpak with Discover.
      2. One line in Konsole adds it to Steam with its own art: how.
      3. The game finds your ROM in EmuDeck's or RetroDECK's folders, or in Downloads.
    • Handhelds with PortMaster AmberELEC, ArkOS, Knulli, muOS, ROCKNIX and the other firmware PortMaster runs on. Made on a Retroid Nova and a Retroid Pocket Flip 2.
      PortMaster zip
      How to install
      1. Unzip it into your handheld's ports/ folder, where PortMaster keeps its ports.
      2. Put your ROM in ports/cyberworld/rom/.
      3. Refresh the game list.
    • New 3DS New 3DS, New 3DS XL and New 2DS XL with custom firmware; the older 3DS and 2DS are too slow for it. Also: .3dsx for the Homebrew Launcher
      CIA
      1. Scan the code with FBI on your 3DS: Remote Install, then Scan QR Code. FBI downloads the game and installs it on the HOME Menu.
      2. Your ROM goes in sdmc:/3ds/cyberworld-endless/rom/, or stays where you keep GBA games (sdmc:/roms/gba/). Any file name works.
      3. The .3dsx instead: copy it into sdmc:/3ds/ and start it from the Homebrew Launcher.

    No install

    • Browser Desktop and phone browsers. The ROM and your saves stay in your browser.
      Play now
      How to play
      1. Choose your ROM on the player's page, and BN5 Team Colonel's beside it if you have it. The next visit starts straight away; Forget ROMs and saves removes them all.
      2. On an iPhone or iPad, add the page to your Home Screen: Safari may clear a site's saves it hasn't seen for seven days.
