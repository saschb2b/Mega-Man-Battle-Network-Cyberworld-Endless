Download

Every build needs your own Mega Man Battle Network 6: Cybeast Gregar (USA) .gba, SHA-1 89fe0bac4fd3d2ab1d2ca35e87ef8b1294a84cd6. None contains game data.

  • Windows installerWindows 10 and 11, 64-bit. For you alone, no administrator: a Start menu entry, uninstalled in Settings. Get
  • Windows zipThe same game, portable: unpack it anywhere and start cyberworld-endless.exe. Get
  • macOS appOne app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 and newer. Get
  • AppImageAny x86-64 Linux: make it executable and start it. It offers to add itself to your menu and to Steam. Get
  • FlatpakInstalls with your software centre (Discover, GNOME Software), sandboxed. The Steam Deck's pick. Get
  • Debian packageDebian, Ubuntu, Mint and their relatives: .deb, installed with the system. Get
  • ArchiveA plain folder, nothing installed: unpack it anywhere (on a Steam Deck, in your home folder) and start it. install.sh adds it to your menu, --add-to-steam to Steam. Get
  • Android appPhones, tablets and Android handhelds, Android 5 and newer. Touch controls, or a controller. Get

  • New 3DS titleA CIA: FBI installs it on the HOME Menu, with its icon and banner. New 3DS, New 3DS XL and New 2DS XL with custom firmware; the older 3DS and 2DS are too slow for it. Get

Start the installer, or unpack the zip. If Windows says it protected your PC: More info, then Run anyway (the game isn't signed).

Drag the app into Applications. It isn't notarized: on its first start, System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.

On first start the game looks for your ROM in Downloads and in EmuDeck's and RetroDECK's folders, or asks for it.

In Desktop Mode, open the Flatpak with Discover. One line in Konsole then adds it to Steam with its own art: how.

Allow your browser or file manager to install the app when Android asks. It asks once for your ROM and keeps a copy beside your saves; uninstalling deletes both.

Unzip into ports/, put your ROM in ports/cyberworld/rom/ and refresh the game list.

Scan the code with FBI on your 3DS: Remote Install, then Scan QR Code. FBI downloads the game and installs it on the HOME Menu.

Your ROM goes in sdmc:/3ds/cyberworld-endless/rom/, or stays where you keep GBA games (sdmc:/roms/gba/). Any file name works.

Choose your ROM on the player's page. The next visit starts straight away; Forget ROM and saves removes both.

New versions: on GitHub, Watch › Custom › Releases, or add the release feed to a reader. Questions, ideas and runs: Discussions.
