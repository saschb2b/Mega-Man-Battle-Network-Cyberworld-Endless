How this works

Every line the game says is here, read from the words files in src/. The catalog is rebuilt whenever a line changes, so what you see is what the game says. Click a chat box to turn its page, as A does in the game. Yellow chips are filled in by the game (a name, a number).

OK keeps a line as it is. Change asks for a rewrite: say what is wrong in the note, or write the wording you want in Suggestion. Cut asks for the line to go. A line reworded after your verdict says changed since your review; one a voice pass rewrote for you says so, and your next verdict on it replaces the old one.

Directions are standing guidance for every line, one speaker or one area, such as "Mr.Prog: fewer exclamations". The ones that hold become rules in docs/VOICE.md, in your words with the date.

Claude reads your open reviews and directions in a voice pass: it rewrites the lines, rebuilds the catalog, republishes this page and marks each item applied. Flags are VOICE.md's mechanical checks, a hint for where to look, never a verdict.

Each review is stored under its line's id (file, function or table, place), which survives rewording. A character a database path cannot hold is written as ~ and its two hex digits.
